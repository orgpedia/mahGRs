Read: - 1. MoU signed by the State Government with the Central Government on procurement of Paddy under the Decentralized Procurement Scheme. Introduction: - The Minimum Support Price Procurement Scheme is a Central Government scheme and is implemented so that farmers do not have to sell their food grains at a distress sale price below the guaranteed price.
For this purpose, FAQ (Fair Average Quality) Tora paddy and millets are procured in the state through agent agencies appointed by the state government (in non-tribal areas, Maharashtra State Cooperative Marketing Federation, Mumbai and tribal areas, Maharashtra State Cooperative Tribal Development Corporation, Nashik). Under the scheme, farmers are registered online every season as per the procedure laid down by the Central Government to benefit the farmers. After this registration, their grains are purchased from the farmers as per the guaranteed price as per the schedule.
Due to various issues arising in farmer registration, delayed procurement process in some places, there was a frequent request from various public representatives, agent agencies, farmers to take measures regarding farmer registration, paddy / millets procurement process to start within the stipulated time. Jhasana was considering issuing a government decision to register farmers who wanted to sell their foodgrains to the government under the Minimum Support Scheme and issuing a comprehensive permanent instruction to set up procurement centres so that they could start farmer registration within the stipulated time frame, set up the required procurement centres and pass on the benefits of the scheme to the common farmers.
