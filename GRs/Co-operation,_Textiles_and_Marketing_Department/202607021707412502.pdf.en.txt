The Proposal: -
The Government has earlier from time to time approved the implementation of a one-time loan repayment scheme as recommended to the Government by the Commissioner and Registrar of Co-operative Societies, Maharashtra State, Pune for effective recovery of unproductive loans of Urban Co-operative Banks in the State.
Several urban cooperative banks had demanded an extension of the one-time loan repayment scheme. Periodic extension of the one-time loan repayment scheme to reduce the rising NPAs of urban cooperative banks has so far helped reduce the rising NPAs of urban cooperative banks. Keeping this in mind, a proposal to extend the one-time loan repayment scheme for urban cooperative banks was under consideration of the government.
# Page 2
The Commissioner and Registrar of Cooperatives, Cooperative Societies, Maharashtra State, Pune has issued Reference No. The government has decided to extend the one-time loan repayment scheme for urban cooperative banks as recommended under letter 19.
The Government's Decisions:
Allow implementation of a one-time loan repayment scheme for urban cooperative banks as mentioned in Annexure 'A' accompanying this Government decision. The deadline is being extended to 31.3.2027. In exercise of the powers conferred by section 157 of the Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act, 1960, to implement the Scheme, the Government hereby exempts the Urban Cooperative Banks of Maharashtra from the provisions of rule 49 of the Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Rules, 1961, for implementing the Scheme subject to the guidelines of RAS Bank of India.
