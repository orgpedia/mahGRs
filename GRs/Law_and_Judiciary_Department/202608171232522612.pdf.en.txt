Read: - 1) ARM Plan of Maharashtra 2047 developed by the Department of Law and Justice
The Proposal: -
The milestones CRAM for the department for the year 2047 under developed Maharashtra 2026-2029 are as follows.
"Establish a State Level Committee for coordination to conduct structural review of prosecutorial functions across the Home and Law & Judiciary Departments. "(To set up a State Level Committee for coordination for structural review of prosecution work in Home and Law & Justice Departments. )
In this regard, a proposal was under consideration of the Government to set up a State Level Coordination Committee consisting of Principal Secretary and Legal Advisor, Department of Law & Justice, Principal Secretary (Special), Home Department, Director General of Police, Director, Directorate of Prosecutions and other concerned officers for periodic review of the prosecutorial functions and inter-departmental coordination.
The Government's Decisions:
A State Level Coordination Committee is being set up for periodic review and inter-departmental coordination of the prosecution functions in this regard as follows. : -
