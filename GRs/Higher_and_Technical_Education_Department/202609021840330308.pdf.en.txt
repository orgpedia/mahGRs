The Proposal:
For academic AT 2026-27, Sankalp Pratishthan, Pune-run Sankalp College of Law, Pune, has filed Writ Petition No. 9595/2026 in Bombay High Court, Bench Bombay, for denial of recognition to the new additional batch of Bachelor of Lounge (LLB) three years in the college. The Bombay High Court, Bench Bombay has passed the final orders in the present writ petition case dated 25.08.2026. Savitribai Phule Pune University, Pune has submitted the proposal of the petitioner institution to the Government for expedited batch approval under the letter No. 3 in reference.
The Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 has come into force with effect from 1st March, 2017. Section 109 (8) of the Act provides for expeditious recognition of additional fresh batches of specific faculties in exceptional circumstances, taking into account the increasing rate of passing examinations in higher secondary schools and the tendency of students towards a particular curriculum, so that no student is deprived of the desired higher education. The criteria and procedures for this are as under No. 2 The Government Decisions dated 04.07.2017 are prescribed. As per the order dated 25.08.2026 dated 25.08.2026 dated No. 4 under the reference of the Hon'ble High Court, the Government was considering expeditiously granting recognition to the new additional batch of Bachelor of Laws (LLB) of Sankalp College of Law, Pune, a college run by Sankalp Pratishthan, Pune for a period of three years.
