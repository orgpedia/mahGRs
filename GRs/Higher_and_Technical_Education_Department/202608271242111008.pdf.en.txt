The Proposal: -
There is a great need for trained social workers to bring the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, women, children, differently-abled and other disadvantaged sections of the society into the mainstream of development. Social work colleges have been started in the state with the objective of meeting this social commitment and the skilled human resources required for preventive and developmental work. At present, there are a total of 69 recognized social work colleges in the state of Maharashtra, out of which 1 is closed, 18 are permanently unaided, 47 are on grant-in-aid, 1 is on partial grant-in-aid and 2 are on grant-in-aid.

Due to the increasing number of social work colleges in the state and the lack of employment opportunities available to that extent, the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance had taken a strategic decision under the Government Decision dated 10.04.2001 and dated 24.08.2004 to disaffiliate the AGH colleges and stop providing Tele-student numbers in the functioning colleges.
However, Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, Government Decision dated 29.02.2024 has transferred the subject "Social Work Colleges" to the Department of Higher and Technical Education.
A committee was constituted under the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government Decision Date 02.09.2024 and Government Supplementary Paper Date 11.09.2024 to study various subjects related to social work colleges. The report submitted by this committee states that the old policy regarding the recognition of new social work colleges needs to be changed. Similarly, on the date 14.05.2026, Ma. The ISH of the Maharashtra State Higher Education and Development Commission (MAHED) was held under the chairmanship of the Chief Minister. Pursuant to the decision taken in these meetings, the Government was under consideration to approve the continuation of TATA as a separate faculty / undergraduate course / additional batch as per the modalities laid down in the Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 in the existing traditional colleges as well as new courses / additional batches in the existing social work colleges on a permanent non-aided basis.
The Government's Decisions:
It is being approved to start a separate faculty / new course / additional batch as per the modalities mentioned in the Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 as well as new courses / additional batch in existing social work colleges on a permanent non-subsidized basis in the existing traditional colleges functioning from these academic years.
2. The institution shall be fully responsible for the creation of new posts in traditional colleges while introducing new courses / additional units in the social work faculty / new courses / additional units in the existing social work colleges on a perpetual non-subsidized basis. Since the approval is on a perpetual non-subsidised basis, the government will not be burdened with any financial liability.
The provisions of Section 107B Section 109 (4) of the Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 and the Government Decision No. 3, dated 218.09.2025 (Procedure B Criteria for Recognition of New Faculty / Course / Subject / Subject / Subject / Substantial Unit under Extension) in respect of "Social Work" as a separate Faculty / New Course / Substantial Unit in functioning traditional colleges and approval for introduction of new STARA SAM Substantial Unit in existing Social Work Colleges shall continue to be applicable with modification of Procedure B Criteria.
