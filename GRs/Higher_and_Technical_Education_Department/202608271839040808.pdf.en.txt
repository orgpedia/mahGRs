Preamble: The Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 has come into force with effect from 01.03.2017. Section 109 (8) of the Act provides for expeditious recognition of additional new batches of specific faculties in exceptional circumstances, taking into account the increasing rate of passing examinations in higher secondary schools and the tendency of students towards a particular curriculum, so that no student is deprived of the desired higher education. In accordance with this provision, proposals for additional new batches of specific faculties were submitted to the government by the state public universities in the academic years 2026-27. Reference No. 2 The inspection was carried out according to the criteria laid down by the government decision here. The government was considering expeditious approval of proposals for additional new batches of specific faculties recommended by the universities to the government, which met the prescribed criteria.
Government Decision: The additional new batches of specific faculties recommended to the Government by the public universities in the State in this academic year are being sanctioned on a perpetual non-subsidized basis in the existing college as per the authority vested in the Government under Section 109 (8) of the Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016 subject to the following terms and conditions as specified in the following statement:
