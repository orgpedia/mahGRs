Introduction: Anna Bhai Sathe has made invaluable and substantial contributions to the Marathi literary world with his supernatural talent. Readers of Anna Bhau's literature in Maharashtra want 1st August to be celebrated as' Writing Inspiration Day 'to commemorate the literary contribution of Anna Bhau Sathe, a world-renowned literary magnate who rose to prominence as a struggling writer across the seas with his pen.
The role behind this is that on the occasion of the birth anniversary of the talented writer of Marathi mind, Anna Bhai Sathe, we should also write and showcase our talent in Marathi literature, this should be the inspiration and talented people from all walks of life should write on this occasion. Accordingly, it was under the consideration of the Government to issue directions to all the Universities / Colleges / Institutions under the Department of Higher and Technical Education to celebrate his birthday on 1st August as' Writing Inspiration Day '.
Government Circular: In commemoration of the literary contribution of the talented Sahityaratna democrat Anna Bhai Sathe, the birthday of the democrat Anna Bhai Sathe is being approved to be celebrated as' Writing Inspiration Day 'in all the Universities / Colleges / Institutions under the Department of Higher and Technical Education on 1st August.
The following activities should be carried out in the above educational institutions on the occasion of this Writing Inspiration Day.
(1) To organize poetry, story, essay writing competition for the students by giving them various subjects.
(2) To organize elocution, debate competitions on various subjects.
(iii) To conduct interviews, lectures with local litterateurs.
(4) To organize Granth Dindi to make the importance of bibliography known.
(v) Reading of Anna bhai's stories, novels, travelogues, folk-dramas and poetry by students.
(6) To organize lectures on the personality and literary achievements of Anna Bhai Sathe.
(7) To organize seminars, symposia, workshops and literary conferences on the literature of Anna Bhai Sathe.
