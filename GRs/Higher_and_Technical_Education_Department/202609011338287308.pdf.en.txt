PREAMBLE
Ministry of Education, Government of India has approved the World Bank
assisted Multidisciplinary Education and Research Improvement in Technical Education
(MERITE) Scheme as a Central Sector Scheme. The Scheme aims to enhance the quality
of teaching and learning, strengthen equitable access to technical education, promote
research, innovation and entrepreneurship through research grants and innovation
ecosystems, and strengthen governance through quality assurance and digitalization
across States/Union Territories.
2. From Maharashtra State, 23 Technical Education institutions have been selected
for participation under the MERITE Scheme. As suggested by The Ministry of
Education, New Delhi, formation of State Steering Committee (SSC) to monitor and
guide the implementation of MERITE Scheme in the State was under consideration of
the Government.

3. Accordingly, in order to provide strategic direction, policy guidance and
effective oversight for implementation of the MERITE Scheme in Maharashtra, the
Government of Maharashtra considers it necessary to constitute a State Steering
Committee (SSC).

2. Tenure and meetings :-
1. The SSC shall function for the duration of the MERITE Scheme, unless
otherwise decided by the Government.
2. The Committee shall meet once in 3 months or as required to review the
progress of implementation of the MERITE Scheme in the State.
3. Role and Functions of the State Steering Committee :-
I. Providing strategic direction and policy guidance to the State Technical
Support Group (STSG) for effective implementation of the Project within the
State.
IJ. Ensuring alignment of Project interventions with State priorities and the
overall objectives of the scheme.
Iii. Providing oversight to promote efficient, transparent, and accountable
implementation.
# Page 4
IV. Reviewing the overall progress and outcomes of the Project and providing
guidance for continuous improvement whenever needed.
V. Addressing key policy and implementation challenges and enabling timely
decision-making.
VI. Supporting institutional strengthening and_ sustainability of reforms
introduced under the Project.
4. The expenditure, if any, required for functioning of the State Steering
Committee shall be met from the provisions available under the MERITE Scheme,
as per the applicable rules and guidelines.
5. This Government Resolution shall come into force with immediate effect.
