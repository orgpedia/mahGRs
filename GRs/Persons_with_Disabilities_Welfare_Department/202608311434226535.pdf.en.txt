The memo: -
The Handbook on "Cross Disability Early Intervention and School Readiness" has been developed by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Government of India in collaboration with FAAS Council of India (RCI).
The main objective of the handbook is to provide uniform guidance on early identification, early intervention (BT), and school readiness in children with disabilities. The distribution of the booklet to Special Schools for Persons with Disabilities, District Differently Abled Rehabilitation Centres (DDRCs), Inclusive Education Resource Centres, Integrated Child Development Scheme is planned to train master trainers in the first phase by conducting workshops for special educators in the state for effective use of the booklet and then conducting this training at the district level to train personnel in activities for persons with disabilities. The training has been accorded administrative approval on 11th August, 2026 as per the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in this regard.
For this training, an amount of Rs.1.00 crore has been provided for the year 2026-27 under the head Training of Government Employees (Programme) (22511372). With this provision, the Finance Department has allocated Rs. 60 lakh has been disbursed. Funds are being disbursed for the booklet launch ceremony, training of master trainers, district level training, etc.
