Government Circular: - The Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities runs special schools, workshops, skill development centres, orphanages, children's homes, etc. for the disabled on a grant basis. These subsidized activities are paid salary, non-salary, and maintenance subsidy as per the norms prescribed by the government.
The Special School Code for Special Schools / Workshops, Training Centres, Affiliated Hostels, Midwifery Homes, Early Diagnosis and Early Treatment Centres for Persons with Disabilities has been implemented by the Government of India vide its decision dated 18th July, 2018. Paragraphs 29 (3) and (4) of Chapter IV of the said School Code provide for grading on the basis of attendance of students in subsidized disabled activities and reduction in the number of staff as per the pattern if the number of students decreases after grading.
The Government Decision dated 16th July, 2024 of the Department lays down the policy for grant of grant-in-aid for permanent unsubsidized / unsubsidized special activities of Divyangjan.
In this Government Decision, Paragraph No. 9 (25) requires that each activity be scheduled annually. Each activity is required to approve staff positions based on the average of student attendance for the months of July, August, and September, as well as payment of maintenance grants, pay / non-payment grants, and building rent.
Also, Government Decision No. EDD-2025 / Q. 236 / A Office of the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities. Revised Procedure for Adjustment of Teachers / Non-Teaching Staff in Special Schools / Workshops for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) under Article 02, dated 30th December, 2025
# Page 2
has been prescribed. As per the said Government decision, the concerned CEO, Zilla Parishad / Collector, Mumbai City / Mumbai Suburban is required to complete the process of enrolment based on the attendance of students in the aided Divyang activities by 15th October every year and submit a report to the Commissioner, Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
Also, if the number of students decreases after enrolment, it is necessary to adjust such additional staff as per the procedure prescribed in the government decision by publishing a list of employees who will be extra as per the pattern.
However, it has been observed that many activities are not scheduled annually under the above provisions. Also, in many of the activities that are scheduled, it is not done according to the prescribed procedure. There is no uniformity in the report to be submitted to the government setting out the activities. Similarly, the adjustment of additional staff at the time of recruitment is also not done within the prescribed time frame.
It is necessary to determine the exact number of students in the aided disabled activities in the state, based on the number of students who are physically present, determine the number of permissible posts accordingly, and distribute the salary and post-paid / maintenance grants from the government subsidy on the same basis. Accordingly, the following notice is being issued.
1) All aided Divyang activities in the state should have enrolment proceedings in accordance with the provisions of the Special Schools Code as well as the Government Decision dated 30th December, 2025. The activities involved in the course determination should include - 1. Number of approved students, 2. Biometric attendance for the months of July, August and September, 3. Number of students in attendance, 4. Student registration on UDISE + system, and 5. Registration of UDID number of students to be verified and corrected.
The process of enrolment should be completed by 15th October every year and the activity-wise report should be filled in the Statement-A and submitted to the Commissioner, Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
If the number of students is found to be less than the sanctioned strength in the recruitment, the number of sanctioned posts should be fixed as per the pattern as per the provisions of the Special School Code and Government Decision dated 30th December, 2025. Accordingly, further action should be taken as per the provisions of the adjustment in the Government Decision by preparing a list of teachers / non-teaching staff who will become redundant.
The salary of the employees working in the posts which are permissible under the 3 EY norms should be paid from the government subsidy. The salary of the employees in the posts which are excess due to recruitment should not be paid from the government subsidy. In case of non-reduction of additional posts on the establishment, the responsibility of paying salaries to the employees of such additional posts shall lie with the organisation concerned.
4) The procedure prescribed in the Government Decision dated 30th December, 2025 should be adopted while adjusting the staff who become surplus after recruitment. Accordingly, the vacancies in the Aided Disability Activities in the same district, if not available, may be filled up at the earliest.

Adjustment proceedings should be carried out for vacancies in the district and subsequently in other districts of the state as required.
5) Appointment to a new post should not be approved until the process of appointment and demotion has been completed. Also, action should not be taken to appoint new staff to the respective vacant post when additional teaching / non-teaching staff is available. The directions dated 30th December, 2025 issued by the Government in this regard should be strictly adhered to.
The report on the UDISE system of student enrolment in all aided disabled activities in the State as per Statement-B and Student Enrolment Report as per Statement-C should be submitted to the Government by the end of the month of October of that year. The report should also be uploaded on the website of the Commissionerate of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities. The report should include the number of students approved, the number of students who attended in person, the biometric attendance, the student record on the SARAL / (11) 1817 system, the student record with a UDID number.
7) Concerned competent authorities shall take necessary action as per the prevailing rules of the Government in respect of activities not furnishing necessary information regarding the number of students, enrolment and placement within the prescribed time frame. The Commissioner, Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Pune, should take action to fix responsibility on the concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officer as well as the concerned officers if it is found that the employees have been paid salaries from the government subsidy without appointment to the post of Apprentice B or in excess of the permissible posts accordingly.
