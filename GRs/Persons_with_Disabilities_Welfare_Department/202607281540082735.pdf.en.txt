The Proposal: -
As per the conflicting complaint of the Board of Directors and the staff of the special venture, The Mancherji Navroji Banaji Andha Udyog Griha Griha, Jogeshwari, Mumbai, the District Collector, Mumbai Suburban, has issued an order regarding the appointment of an administrator on the venture as the management is not able to run the venture and the day-to-day running of the workshop is difficult due to mismanagement. A letter dated 30 April 2026 recommended to the Commissioner, Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities. Accordingly, the Commissioner, Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Pune has agreed to the recommendation made by the Collector, Mumbai Suburban. Also, it is recommended that a decision be made by giving the initiative an opportunity to have a say before appointing an administrator on the activity concerned.
The Government decided to appoint administrators for the management of special schools / workshops for the disabled run by NGOs. As per the provision dated 09.03.2004 as well as the provision in Part III (84) (I) of the Special Schools for Disabled Activities Code-2018, the Government shall have the power to appoint an administrator over the institution until such time as the management is satisfied that the management is competent to provide facilities to the students with disabilities and to take over the management of such institution and improve the functioning of the school or until the difference in management is resolved, but the tenure of the administrator shall not exceed three years, whichever is earlier. Accordingly, the matter of appointing an administrator on the initiative was under the consideration of the government.
