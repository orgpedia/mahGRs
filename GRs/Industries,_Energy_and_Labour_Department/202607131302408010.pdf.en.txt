The Proposal: -
The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 was enacted by the Central Government to regulate the conditions of employment and conditions of service of workers working in the building and other construction sectors as well as to provide for safety, health and welfare measures for construction workers. Accordingly, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 2007 were framed to give effect to the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996. Under Section 18 of the Act, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board has been established in the state.
As per Section 3 (1) of the Building and Other Construction Workers Cess Act, 1996, the Central Government by notification dated 26.06.1996 has fixed a cess of 1 per cent of the total project cost (excluding land value). Accordingly, workers working in building and other construction sectors in the state.

Various welfare schemes are being implemented by the Board for the registered active (living) construction workers out of the grant-in-aid funds that are renewed and deposited with the Board.
The central government has repealed 29 labour laws and started the implementation of four new labour codes across the country from the date 21.11.2025. Under Reference No. The Act at 1B2 has been repealed and now matters relating to building and other construction workers welfare have been included in the "Social Security Code-2020."
Section (7) (6) of the said Code specifies the provisions as to the matters for which various welfare schemes are required to be implemented by the construction workers. Accordingly, section (7) (6) (t) provides for "frame educational schemes for the benefit of children of the parents as may be prescribed by the appropriate Government."
Pursuant to these provisions, the Board in its meeting dated 12.05.2026 passed a resolution vide Reference No. Under Section 9, a proposal has been submitted to the government regarding the implementation of various educational assistance schemes to the first two children of living (active) construction workers registered under the Board.
Under the Right to Education Act, 2009, free education is provided to all children from the age of six to six years (classes I to VIII). Thus, while they do not need assistance in the form of direct subsidies, they do need other basic items. The financial burden of education for children after Class VIII falls on construction workers. Class X and Class XII are the two important stages in a child's education, so special care must be taken to ensure that their education is not interrupted. Considering all these factors, various age-wise and child-future considerations, the Government was contemplating to implement a revised "Education Assistance Scheme" for the first two children of living (active) construction workers registered under the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai, in accordance with the said provision in the Social Security Code, 2020 by scrapping the existing schemes for the first two children of construction workers previously registered with the Board.
Government Decision: - Under this Government Decision, it is being approved to implement the revised "Educational Assistance Scheme" in line with the said provision in the Social Security Code, 2020 by discontinuing all earlier ongoing educational assistance schemes for the first two batches of living (active) construction workers registered under the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai.
Accordingly, seven educational items are covered under the scheme, for which the name of the scheme, eligibility criteria, financial assistance, and necessary documents are mentioned in the accompanying "Form-A."
The selection of materials required for the basic goods cycle will be finalised by the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Government of Mumbai with approval.
Expenditure on the scheme should be met from the cess collected with the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai.
