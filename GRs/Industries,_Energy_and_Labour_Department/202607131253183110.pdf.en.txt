The Proposal: -
The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 was enacted by the Central Government to regulate the conditions of employment and conditions of service of workers working in the building and other construction sectors as well as to provide for safety, health and welfare measures for construction workers. Accordingly, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 2007 were framed to give effect to the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996. Under Section 18 of the Act, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board has been established in the state.
As per Section 3 (1) of the Building and Other Construction Workers Cess Act, 1996, the Central Government by notification dated 26.06.1996 has fixed a cess of 1 per cent of the total project cost (excluding land value). Accordingly, workers working in building and other construction sectors in the state.

Various welfare schemes are being implemented by the Board for the registered active (living) construction workers out of the grant-in-aid funds that are renewed and deposited with the Board.
The central government has repealed 29 labour laws and started the implementation of four new labour codes across the country with effect from 21 November 2025. Under Reference No. The Act at 1B2 has been repealed and now matters relating to building and other construction workers welfare have been included in the "Social Security Code-2020."
Section (7) (6) of the said Code specifies the provisions for the purpose for which various welfare schemes are required to be implemented by the construction workers. Accordingly, Section (7) (6) (a) HA provides for "providing death and disability benefits to a beneficiary or his dependents."
Pursuant to these provisions, the Board in its meetings dated 12.05.2026 passed a resolution vide Reference No. A proposal has been submitted to the Government for introduction of a new Death Assistance and Disability Assistance Scheme for living (active) construction workers registered under the Board under Section 9. A construction worker is working in a hazardous area, where they are killed or RATT in an accident at a construction site. In such a situation, to support them and their dependents, it was under the consideration of the government to abolish the existing scheme for construction workers registered by the Board earlier and implement the revised "Death Assistance B Divyang Assistance Scheme" as per the provisions mentioned in the Social Security Code.
Government Decision: - Under Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai, the ongoing (Funeral Assistance-Rs. 10,000 / One-time (F-06), heir assistance - Rs. 24,000 / - per annum for five years (F-07) and the Post-mortem-Factual Expenditure (F-08) Scheme as well as discontinuation of the ongoing Artificial Limbs for Disabled-Factual Expenditure (H-07) Scheme and implementation of the revised "Death Assistance and Disability Assistance Scheme" to the registered living (active) construction workers is being approved by this Government.
Accordingly, the three items covered under the scheme are Natural Death Assistance Scheme, Accidental Death Assistance Scheme, Disability Assistance Scheme, for which the name of the scheme, eligibility criteria, financial assistance and necessary documents are mentioned in the accompanying "Form-A."
The cost of the scheme should be met from the cess collected with the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai.
