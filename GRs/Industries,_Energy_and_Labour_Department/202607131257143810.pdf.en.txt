Various welfare schemes are being implemented by the Board for the registered active (living) construction workers out of the grant-in-aid funds that are renewed and deposited with the Board.
The central government has repealed 29 labour laws and started the implementation of four new labour codes across the country from the date 21.11.2025. Under Reference No. The Act at 1B2 has been repealed and now matters relating to building and other construction workers welfare have been included in the "Social Security Code-2020."
Section (7) (6) of the said Code provides for the purposes for which various welfare schemes are required to be implemented by the construction workers. Accordingly, in section (7) (6) 7, "make payment of pension to the employees who have completed the age of sixty years." "This provision has been mentioned.
Pursuant to these provisions, the Board in its meetings dated 12.05.2026 passed a resolution vide Reference No. A proposal to implement a pension scheme for construction workers registered under Section 9 has been submitted to the government. After attaining the age of 60 years, the construction worker is not registered with the Board and his previous registration is not renewed. As a result, the worker is deprived of various schemes from the board.
In such a situation, to help them contribute to the ease of living in their old age, the government was considering implementing a revised "Pension Scheme" as per the said provision in the Social Security Code by discontinuing the existing scheme of pension for construction workers registered by the Board earlier.
Government Decision: - For construction workers registered under the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai, on completion of 60 AT of their age, the pension scheme previously implemented shall be abolished and the amount paid per annum as per the aforesaid provision in the Social Security Code shall be Rs. The revised "Pension Scheme" for payment of pension up to Rs.12,000 / - is being approved under this Government Decision.
(9) Eligibility Criteria for Pension Scheme: - (a) All construction workers who have completed 10 years of age and are registered with the Board for at least 10 consecutive years shall be eligible under the scheme. Also, if both the husband and wife of his family are construction workers, they will be eligible separately.
(b) On the death of the spouse, the spouse of the construction worker concerned shall be eligible for pension. However, if the spouse is receiving a pension under the scheme, the relative will not get the benefit of double pension.
(a) As per the guidelines of the Model Welfare Schemes of the Central Government, construction workers receiving benefits under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 and the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 shall not be eligible for pension.
