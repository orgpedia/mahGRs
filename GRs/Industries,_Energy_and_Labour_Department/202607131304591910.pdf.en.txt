The Proposal: -
The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 was enacted by the Central Government to regulate the conditions of employment and conditions of service of workers working in building and other construction sectors as well as to provide for safety, health and welfare measures for construction workers. Accordingly, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 2007 were framed to give effect to the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996. Under Section 18 of the Act, the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board has been established in the state.
As per Section 3 (2) of the Building and Other Construction Workers Cess Act, 1996, the Central Government by notification dated 26.06.1996 has fixed a cess of 1 per cent of the total project cost (depreciation of land value). through the Board of Workers working in the building and other construction sectors in the state.

The central government has repealed 29 labour laws and started the implementation of four new labour codes across the country from the date 21.11.2025. Under Reference No. The Act at 1B2 has been repealed and now matters relating to building and other construction workers welfare have been included in the "Social Security Code-2020."
Section (7) (6) of the said Code specifies the provisions as to the matters for which various welfare schemes are required to be implemented by the construction workers. Accordingly, in section (7) (6) (0), "make payment of maternity benefit to the parents." "This provision has been mentioned.
Pursuant to these provisions, the Board in its meeting dated 12.05.2026 passed a resolution vide Reference No. A proposal to implement Maternity Benefit Scheme for living (active) women construction workers registered under the Board under Section 10 has been submitted to the Government. Female construction workers are unable to go to work after this delivery due to childcare responsibilities. Therefore, the Government had earlier issued the Government's Communication No. 1 to support women workers in the construction sector for their maternity economic safety and health, as well as to prevent malnutrition of AA-AA children. The government was now considering implementing the revised "Maternity Benefit Scheme" under the said mentioned provision in the Social Security Code, which was discontinued under letter 5.
Government Decision: - Under this Government Decision, the implementation of the revised "Maternity Benefit Scheme" is being approved for the living (active) women construction workers registered under the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai. The eligibility criteria for the scheme, financial assistance, and required documents are mentioned in the accompanying "Form-A."
Expenditure on the scheme should be met from the cess collected with the Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Mumbai.
