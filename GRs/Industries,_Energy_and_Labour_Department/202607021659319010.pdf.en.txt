The Government's Decisions:
The government has formulated the Grape Processing Industry Policy-2001 to promote the cultivation of grapes for the benefit of the grape farmers of the state, making dry fruits and producing economically more profitable alternative products from grapes, as well as promoting the grape-based wine industry. The rule of 19.10.2001 is declared by decree.
As part of the implementation of this policy, the government has introduced a scheme to provide a biannual incentive subsidy equivalent to 16% of the value-added tax payable to the wine industry on sale of 20% of the final wine produced and sold in the state. The rule of 31.08.2009 has been implemented as per the decision of the Hon'ble Cabinet in its meeting held on 04.01.2024 and 14.02.2024.
