The Proposal: -
The reservoir of Koyna Dam is being called "Shivaji Ae Reservoir." to Shivaji Maharaj.
It is respectfully referred to as "Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj." Hence, the Shivaji Sagar of the Koyna Dam reservoir.
Instead, the government received a request to rename "Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Koyna AGA."
It had happened.
Subsequently, the reservoir of Koyna Dam was renamed as Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Koyna Jal Sagar *.
A proposal to rename was under the consideration of the government.
The Government's Decisions:
Koyna Dam reservoir renamed as "Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Koyna Jal Sagar"
It's coming.
