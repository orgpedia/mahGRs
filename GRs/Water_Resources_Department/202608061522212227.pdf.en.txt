Introduction: Lakh Bandhara is 45 km downstream of the Ozar Dam on the Pravara River near Lakh village.
It's far away. The dam was constructed in 1867. The total length of the dam is 393 m with a height of 4. 75 m and a storage capacity of 25 dLh ft. Lakh canal has a capacity of 80 cusecs with a project irrigation area of 6600. Some of the other areas were irrigated by the embankments of K.P. Bandhara Malunje, ASM, Vangi, Pachegaon, Punatgaon, Madhyameswar (Nevasa) on the WAT river
The dam has water supply through lakhs of canals through which water is blocked during the monsoons in Malunja, Ladgaon, Berdapur of Shrirampur taluka, Pachegaon, Punatgaon, Nevasa Bu, Bahirwadi of Vangi B Nevasa taluka, etc.
The benefit areas of the villages were supplied with water during the Kharif and Rabi seasons. The canal is drawn from the left bank of the dam and is 37 km long.
The bioman of the Lakh Bandhara project is more than 150 years old. The distribution system is in complete disarray. Grouting is necessary as the dam is leaking. The canal needs to be restored as its carrying capacity is low.

Once the dam is repaired, the dam is going to be strengthened, the canal's carrying capacity will be flooded and the water flowing during the monsoon season will be able to supply water to the agricultural and rural areas. Also, the 105.00 is when this task is completed. The irrigation area is to be restored.
Against this backdrop, lakhs of people have been affected. A proposal was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval under a special amendment to the budget for the dam B canal repair work at Ahilyanagar, Srirampur District.
