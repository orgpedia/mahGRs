The Proposal:
Patgaon Medium Project, Ta. The Bhudargarh, District Kolhapur project is built on the Vedganga River. or
The main dam and 17 kopur dams are under the project. The project was completed in 2007.
Water is being stored at capacity. The total storage capacity of the project is 105.242 Dalhousie.
Water is blocked in these dams and water is supplied for agriculture through private irrigation.
The project has submerged an area of about 8,100 hectares in Bhudargarh taluka.
The use of micro-irrigation during the Rabi season saves an average of 1.68 million litres of water.
It has been decided. Out of this saving water, 1.68 lakh litres is proposed to be supplied by the Water Conservation Department.
Re-planning of Patgaon Medium Project for Reservation for Devkewadi Subsidiary Irrigation Scheme
Letter of Reference No. Submitted under 01.
Accordingly, the re-watering of the Patgaon Medium Project was under the consideration of the government.
Accordingly, the following Government Decisions are being issued.
The Government's Decisions:
1) The Executive Director and the Chief Engineer will be fully responsible for the re-water planning of the Patgaon Medium Project submitted by the Corporation.
The Executive Director and the Chief Engineer shall be responsible for ensuring that there is no change in the water reserve for the irrigated area which is not yet fully developed out of the total benefit area of the project.
3) The Integrated State Water Plan should be suitably modified in accordance with this plan.
4) If the project area needs to be notified / de-notified due to this re-watering, appropriate action should be taken.
