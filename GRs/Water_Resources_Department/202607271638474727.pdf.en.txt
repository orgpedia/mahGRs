The Proposal: -
Maharashtra Irrigation System is governed by the provisions of the Maharashtra Management of Minor, Medium, and Large Scale Irrigation Schemes Act, 2005. As per the Act, it is mandatory for all the above types of irrigation projects to have a beneficiary farmer's water use agency to complete the projects and distribution system and transfer the benefit area to the beneficiary water use agency through the Water Resources Department. Further, after the transfer of the benefit area to the water use agency, the water use agency is expected to carry out irrigation management, distribution system maintenance repair, water banding and recovery, etc.
It is expected to be operationalized by setting up small distribution level water utility initially followed by high level distribution, canal and project level water utility in a phased manner.
2. The government is trying to manage irrigation through martyrs in various ways. About 4500 water utility units have finally been established in the state today. But not much progress is seen in setting up water use agencies. The main reasons for this are - 1) Lack of interest of officers / staff in irrigation management.
2) Lack of functioning of a mechanism to check whether the above policies are effectively implemented by the government / corporation.
3) Less involvement of members, office bearers and farmers in training, awareness, publicity, etc.
3. To focus on the above aspects and to determine the modalities for regulation, evaluation, training and guidance of water use agencies through the field establishment of water resources.
