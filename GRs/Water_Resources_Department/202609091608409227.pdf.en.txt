Government of Maharashtra, WRD has issued comprehensive Policy dt. 20.12.2023
vide ref. (1) above for development of PSPs through Public Private Partnership (PPP) &
subsequent corrigendum to this policy vide ref. (2) regarding signing of MoU by developer
with GOMWRD.
In order to bring more clarity regarding clauses especially related to "Right of First
Refusal, "The following corrigendum is issued to the Government Resolution No MISC
2021/ CR82/2021/HP, dt. 20.12.2023.
1) In clause A-I.16 under Part A: Implementation Framework, A-I Implementation
of Category- I (a, b & c) PSPs :-
"The" Right of First Refusal "for the purchase of energy generated shall vest with the state distribution licensee. The developer has to arrange for the pumping energy. No capital investment will be made or revenue expenditure will be incurred on any component of the project by GOMWRD / IDC. "
Shall read as
The 'Right of First Refusal' for the purchase of energy generated shall vest with the state distribution licensee, subject to the condition that the distribution licensee shall
communicate its decision within 90 days from date of communication by the
developer. In the event that no decision 1s communicated within the stipulated period
of 90 days, the same shall be deemed to constitute refusal by the Distribution
Licensee. Developer has to arrange for the pumping energy. No capital investment
# Page 2
will be made or revenue expenditure will be incurred on any component of the project by GoMWRD / IDC "
2) In clause A-II.13 under Part A: Implementation Framework, A-II
Implementation of Category- II PSPs:-
The "Right of First Refusal" for the purchase of energy generated shall vest with the state distribution licensee. The developer has to arrange for the pumping energy. No capital investment will be made or revenue expenditure will be incurred on any component of the project by GOMWRD / IDC. "
Shall read as
The 'Right of First Refusal' for the purchase of energy generated shall vest with the state distribution licensee, subject to the condition that the distribution licensee shall communicate
its decision within 90 days from date of communication by the developer. In the event
that no decision is communicated within the stipulated period of 90 days, the same shall
be deemed to constitute a refusal by the Distribution Licensee. The developer has to arrange for the pumping energy. No capital investment will be made or revenue expenditure will be incurred on any component of the project by GOMWRD / IDC. "
3) In clause D-II Sale of Energy under PART-D: Generation, sale of Energy &
Regulatory Provisions :-
"Energy storage capacity of PSP can be integrated with any solar and / or wind projects located in any part of India including the state of Maharashtra, for delivering firm Renewable Energy (RE). The distribution licensee (s) in the state are not obligated
to purchase the power from the PSP. Similarly, they are not obligated to supply pumping energy to the PSP. However, distribution licensee(s) shall have "First Right of Refusal" for all the benefits of the PSP. However, distribution licensee shall
communicate its decision within 30 days from date of communication by the developer.
The tariff for sale of power and ancillary services provided by the PSP to the distribution licensee shall be governed by the applicable regulations of the Appropriate
Commission. "
Shall read as
"Energy storage capacity of PSP can be integrated with any solar and / or wind projects located in any part of India including the state of Maharashtra, for delivering firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy (FDRE) or any such arrangement. The distribution licensee (s) in the state are not obligated to purchase the power from the PSP. Similarly, they are not obligated to supply pumping energy to the PSP.
Distribution licensee(s) shall have "First Right of Refusal" for all the benefits of the
PSP. However, distribution licensee shall communicate its decision within 90 days
from date of communication by the developer. In the event that no decision 15
communicated within the stipulated period of 90 days, the same shall be deemed to
constitute refusal by the Distribution Licensee. The tariff for sale of power and ancillary
# Page 3
services provided by the PSP to the distribution licensee shall be governed by the
Applicable regulations of the Appropriate Commission. "
4) In clause E-II.2 under PART-E: Co-located PSP- Solar and Other RE
Hybrid Projects :-
"On scrutiny of such proposal and subject to availability of additional land, the
proposal will be approved and the separate Letter of Permission (LoP) shall be issued
by the CE (Electrical), Hydro Projects, Mumbai with prior approval of the
GoMWRD / IDC. For other Renewable Energy (RE) set up the permission shall be obtained from the concerned competitive authority. "
Shall read as
"On scrutiny of such proposal and subject to availability of additional land, the
proposal will be approved and the separate Letter of Permission (LoP) shall be issued
by the CE (Electrical), Hydro Projects, Mumbai with prior approval of the
GoMWRD / IDC. For other Renewable Energy (RE) set up the permission shall be obtained from the concerned Competent Authority. "
