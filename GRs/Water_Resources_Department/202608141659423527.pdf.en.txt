The Proposal: -
Government Decisions, Government Circulars, Orders and Guidelines have been issued by the Department of Water Resources on various matters / subjects related to irrigation management as per the time.
Since then, a number of policy changes, changes in laws / regulations, technical reforms, as well as revised government decisions have been issued as per administrative requirements. Over time, various government decisions / circulars, orders have been issued and several government decisions regarding the same subject appear to exist. It is also found that some of the provisions in the Government Decision are repetitive and some of the provisions are obsolete. Under the principle of "One Subject-One Updated and Consolidated Government Decision," a separate committee was under consideration to recommend to the Government, subject-wise compilation, examination, deletion of obsolete provision and consolidation of applicable provisions in respect of Government Decisions / Circulars, Orders relating to irrigation management.
The Government's Decisions:
The following Committee is being constituted to consolidate and review the Government decisions / circulars / orders on irrigation management.

Scope of work of the Committee: - 1. To compile all Government decisions, circulars and orders relating to irrigation management.
2. Subject-wise and chronological classification.
3. Determination of applicable, partially applicable and obsolete Government decisions.
4. Check compatibility with existing laws, regulations and applicable provisions of MWRRA.
5. To prepare a consolidated draft Government Decision for each major subject.
