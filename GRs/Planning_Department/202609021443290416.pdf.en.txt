Government circular:
The Commissionerate of Finance and Statistics, Mumbai has taken up the process of updating the entire database of all employees in the service of the Government of Maharashtra on an annual basis. The database will collect information on all types of employees, from regular establishment employees to non-regular establishment employees (working establishment, daily wage, part-time, volunteer, etc.) as well as employees who have been appointed on an ad-hoc basis. Based on that, the "Employees Master Database * (EMDb) of MAHA employees" is being updated, which includes information on all filled and vacant posts of government employees. In accordance with that. LD for employee service as of the reference date of July 1, 2026. , the employee's full name, date of birth, gender, designation, date of joining service, date of retirement, designation of joining service, provident fund / DCPS account number, PAN number, Aadhaar number, employee's mobile number, employee's email 1. Information such as permanent status, social category, employee's caste, religion, identity, person with disability, etc., as well as (if applicable) current designation, date of promotion to the current position, assured progress plan, etc. will be collected. Further, the full month of July, 2026, will also be covered by the details of the fiscal year. The Commissionerate of Finance and Statistics will provide the relevant offices with a computerized online command line for information registration, as well as a set of instructions for using login IDs and passwords, and a command line for information entry.

2. Instructions to Drawing and Disbursing Officers
a) Every Drawing and Disbursing Officer in the State Government offices should obtain online AMAA log-in-ID and password from the respective Regional / District Statistics Offices. For this database, provide information about the employees of the respective office. Information filled in by their office in the Employee Master Database-2025 will be made available as a basis for updating as of the reference date of July 1, 2026. A set of instructions for filling out the information will be made available in the online command line.
b) Drawing and Disbursing Officer-wise information of State Government offices is being made available in online format for updating the concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officers. The employees whose information is included in the support information are called. The information of all such employees falling under the jurisdiction of the concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officers as on the reference date of 1st July, 2026 is to be updated in the Computer Online Orders. In addition, information about new employees (such as those who have been transferred or newly hired, etc.) and supporting information is not included, however, in the data. The information of employees under the jurisdiction of the Drawing and Disbursing Officer as on 9th July, 2026 is also to be filled in the online order issued by this Commissionerate.
e) In the case of employees who took voluntary retirement in the month of July, if it is not paid. If there are employees of the state government on July 9, 2026, then such employees should also be included in the database as government funds were spent on them in the month of July. While registering the information in the commandment, make sure that the information is being recorded according to the employee's service record.
e) The information of the employees is to be duly registered by following the instructions provided in the online instructions set as well as in the user manual. The concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officer is to take care of registering the information of all the employees who come to the premises while registering the information. In this way, a certificate of receipt of information after completing the online registration is made available by the District Statistical Office / Regional Office to the concerned Drawing and Disbursing Officer.
