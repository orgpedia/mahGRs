Also, the Director, Directorate of Other Backward Classes Bahujan Kalyan, Pune should take care that after the student applies for the post-matric scholarship scheme on the NSP (National Scholarship Portal) portal, the same student will not apply for the UAT twice on the MahaDBT portal.

6. In pursuance of the above reference, MAHALT, Mumbai has been informed to urgently develop a technical system to prevent duplication of application by providing data from the NSP through API (Application Programming Interface) or to validate the technical requirement that a student who has applied for a post-matric scholarship scheme on NSP (National Scholarship Portal) should not apply for a refresher twice on the MahaDBT portal. The funds should be spent only after the Director, Other Backward Classes Bahujan Kalyan, Pune, in coordination with MahaIT, Mumbai, immediately develops appropriate measures / systems to ensure that the student applications do not become ESHAs.
