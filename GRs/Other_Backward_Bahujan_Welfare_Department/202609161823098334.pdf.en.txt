The Proposal: -
The mother of our country. On the occasion of completion of 25 years of Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi's tenure in the constitutional office, the Department of General Administration, vide Reference No. 2 by the decision of the Government here. September 97, 2026 to d. It has been decided to implement "Seva Sankalp Abhiyan" in the state during 97th October, 2026. This government decision has directed to take up various activities with the active participation of school students. These include painting and elocution competitions, "Namo Sevakatha" programmes, especially based on the theme of "Developed India," as well as activities related to education, career guidance, digital literacy, and providing necessary certificates to students. Accordingly, various activities were under the consideration of the government to be implemented in the primary, secondary and higher secondary, sugarcane harvesting and Vidyaniketan Ashramshalas of the Vijabhabhaj category under the Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare Department.
Government Circular: -
The following activities should be carried out to implement the "Seva Sankalp Abhiyan" in the Vija Bhajan Ashramshalas under the Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare Department. : -
2) "Developed India-2047" Painting and Elocution Competition: -
9. Painting Competition All ashramshalas should conduct painting competitions based on the theme "Developed India" for students. The competition should encourage students to share their ideas through themes such as developed India, nation-building, concept of New India, clean and empowered India, environment conservation, technology, education, skills and employment, etc. A school-level exhibition of the best paintings selected in the competition should be held.

1.2 Speech Competition
An elocution competition should be held for students on the following topics: - "India of my dreams."
"Developed India - 2047"
The teacher should explain to the students the various public welfare schemes of the government as well as the concept of developed India.
1.3 Selection to the next level
The winners of the best picture and elocution competition should be selected from the school level. The lecture presentations of the selected students should be recorded in video format and submitted to the respective Regional Deputy Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare through the respective Assistant Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare. Presentation of the winners of the elocution competition and video exhibition of the best paintings at the district level should be organized by all the Assistant Directors and the state level exhibition should be organized by the Director, Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare Directorate, Pune (State Nodal Officer). Also, meritorious students should be given appropriate citations / awards.
Namo Sevagatha School / District Level Programmes: -
On 2nd October, 2026, "Namo Sevatha" program should be organized in every district. Through art troupes / theatre troupes, information about various public welfare schemes of the central and state governments should be made available to students, parents and citizens. The programme will include participation of local artists, cultural programmes by students, informative presentations on government schemes, and felicitation of outstanding students and teachers. The District Level Nodal Officer for the programme will be the respective Assistant Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare. The compiled report is submitted to the Department of Other Backward Bahujan Welfare through the respective Regional Deputy Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare.

4) "Developed India-2047" awareness in Ashramshalas: -
In ashramshalas, special sessions should be conducted and guided to make students aware of the concept of developed India-2047. It should focus on: - Quality education

5) "Seva Sankalp" in Ashramshalas: -
During the "Seva Sankalp Abhiyan," every ashramshala should implement at least one meaningful service initiative keeping in mind their local needs. Students, teachers, parents, school management committee, alumni and local citizens should participate in this activity. At the end of the activity, the student asks, "What can I do for the community?" "Service resolutions should be made based on this spirit.

6) Publicity and documentation: -
Photography of all activities, video shooting, student experiences / feedback, success stories of outstanding activities should be compiled. The best photos / videos / reels received should be used for publicity on the official social media of the department. Also, the nodal officer appointed for the purpose is expected to compile videos, rolls and photographs of such works and make them available to the Information and Public Relations Department.
Implementation and control: -
Ashramshala Level: - The headmaster will be the coordinator of school level activities.
District Level: - The Assistant Director of the concerned district, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare will act as the nodal officer at the district level. They will guide ashramshalas, regulate activities, select best practices, prepare district-level reports.
Section Level: - The Regional Deputy Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare concerned will act as the Section Level Coordinator. They will compile district-wise data and submit departmental reports to the Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare Department through a state-level nodal.
State Level: - Director, Directorate of Other Backward Classes Bahujan Kalyan, Maharashtra State, Pune will act as the nodal officer for implementation of "Seva Sankalp Abhiyan" throughout the state.
Presentation of the report: -
Each school should keep a brief account of the activity undertaken, the number of students / teachers involved, photographs and videos as required. The Assistant Director should prepare a district-wise compiled report and submit it to the Regional Deputy Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare. The Regional Deputy Director, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare shall submit the district-wise compiled report to the Other Backward Bahujan Welfare Department through the Director, Directorate of Other Backward Bahujan Welfare, Pune.
9) The time limit: -
All the activities. September 97, 2026 to d. 97% to be implemented during 22nd October. On 2nd October, 2026, "Namo Sevakatha" programme will be required to be organized at district level.
10) Suggestions to all concerned: -
The Director, Directorate of Other Backward Bahujan Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune, all Regional Deputy Directors, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare, Assistant Directors, Principals of Other Backward Bahujan Welfare and Ashramshalas should ensure the active participation of students in this campaign, the activities should be not only formal but should also inculcate a sense of service, social responsibility, and nation-building among the students.
