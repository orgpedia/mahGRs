The Proposal: -
Scheme-wise funds have been budgeted for payment of post-matric scholarships and tuition fees and examination fees to the students belonging to the Denotified Castes, Nomadic Tribes, Special Backward Classes and Other Backward Classes during this additional year. It was under the consideration of the Government to disburse the funds made available by the Department of Finance on the BEAMS system to the Director, Directorate of Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare, Pune.
Government Decision: - The budgetary provision for the year 2026-27 plus the budgetary provision for the year 100% plus the monsoon session in July, 2026 approved by the Purbani Demand out of the funds made available through the Budget Estimates, Allocation and Control System (on the BIMSTEC system) is Rs. The Government is approving the disbursement of funds to the tune of Rs. 990.00 crore (Rupees Two Hundred and Ten Crore only) to the Director, Directorate of Other Backward Classes Bahujan Welfare, Pune.

Also, the available provision should be distributed to the eligible students through the MahaDBT portal by following the prescribed procedure. Accordingly, the terms and conditions issued regarding disbursement of funds through the MahaDBT portal should be strictly adhered to.
