The Proposal: -
It is essential to streamline the day-to-day and policy functioning of AMRUT, maintain financial discipline and ensure timely disposal of statutory matters. Accordingly, the government was considering setting up a competent executive committee to regulate and supervise the functioning of the institution. Accordingly, the government has decided to set up an executive committee for AMRUT and fix its functions.
The Government's Decisions:
The following Executive Committee is being constituted for the management of AMRUT and its composition, convening of meetings and responsibilities / functions are being defined as follows:

Schedule of meetings:
The Chairman of the Committee (Deputy Director, Yashada) will be mandated to hold bi-monthly review meetings of the Executive Committee. It is also expected to hold at least one meeting every month at the level of other members.
Functions and Responsibilities of the Executive Committee:
The Executive Committee shall perform the following main functions:
The first and foremost function of the committee shall be to prepare the annual and quarterly budget of the institution.
2. Submit report of the functioning and progress of the institution to the Governing Body (Governing 8015 / Governing Body) for approval.
3. To closely monitor and control all statutory compliances relating to AMRUT.
4. Review of all expenditure MIM (expenditure heads) and account-wise details. Verifying the balance of the institution's Institutional Bank Accounts on the date of review.
5. Prepare the annual balance sheet of the institution and further supervise its accuracy.
