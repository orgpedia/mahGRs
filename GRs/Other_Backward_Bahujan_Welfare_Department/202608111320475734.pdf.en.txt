The Proposal: -
The powers and duties of the Board of Regulators were required to be clearly defined in order to streamline the day-to-day and strategic functioning of AMRUT as well as to effectively implement the objectives of the Institute. In this regard, the following decisions were under consideration at the government level.
The Government's Decisions:
The powers and duties of the Governing Council of AMRUT are as follows: 9. Approval of the Annual Budget: - Final approval of the comprehensive annual budget plan and planned provisions for the next additional year of the institute.
Conducting internal audits: - Regularly to maintain transparency and financial discipline in the overall economic affairs of the institution.
Conducting and controlling internal audits.
3. Launching new initiatives that meet the aspirations of AMRUT beneficiaries: - Approving the launch of new schemes, projects or initiatives for the overall welfare and development of AMRUT beneficiaries keeping in mind their needs, expectations and aspirations.
4. Evaluation and approval of quarterly expenditure of AMRUT: -
A quarterly review of the expenditure incurred / to be incurred by AMRUT and sanction of related expenditure matters.
Audit paragraphs and approval of compliance.-
Acknowledging the organization's annual or special report. Also, revise the paragraphs of the audit report and approve the compliance report made thereon.

6. Approval of Financial Report prepared by AMRUT: -
To examine and give official approval to the final financial reports, balance sheets and accounts prepared by the management of AMRUT.
