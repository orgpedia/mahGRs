The Proposal: -
The government was considering promoting and publicising these schemes in line with the Maharashtra State Other Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation's plan to reach out to the last mile of Other Backward Classes and its newly established subsidiaries to achieve the SENS set out in the Additional Annual Plan of the corporation.
The Government's Decisions:
The Directorate General of Public Relations has prepared a roadmap for dissemination and dissemination of information about the schemes of the Maharashtra State Other Backward Classes Finance and Development Corporation and its newly established subsidiaries to reach out to the district / taluka / rural areas and this roadmap has been approved by the Media Planning and Implementation Committee in its meetings dated 15th October, 2025. Accordingly, the Government is giving administrative approval for advertisement and dissemination of detailed information about the schemes through durable colour printed ACP Display Boards (including UV Printing) through Kiosk, electronic media as well as through social media platforms like YouTube, Facebook, Instagram as per Annexure-A.
