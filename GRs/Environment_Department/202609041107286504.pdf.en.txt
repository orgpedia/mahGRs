The procedure for the award of cash prizes to the winning local bodies in the 12 categories of the award has been notified vide Government Decision dated 27.09.2024 at Reference No. (1). Accordingly, a Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken up by the local bodies out of the award amount has been prepared vide Government Decision dated 08.02.2022 at Reference No. Administrative approval has to be obtained from a committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change.
2. Accordingly, for grant of administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report received from the Awardee Local Bodies, Ms. The Committee has made recommendations after discussing the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Local Bodies in its meetings held on 07.04.2026 under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change. It was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report of the respective local bodies as recommended by the committee.
Government Decision: Taking into consideration the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of Nimbargi Gram Panchayat under Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 costing Rs.15,02, 633.00 (Rupees Fifteen Lakh Two Thousand Six Hundred and Thirty Three Only) out of the total prize money of Rs. 15.00 lakh received by Nimbargi Gram Panchayat was submitted by the Village Development Officer, Nimbargi Gram Panchayat to the Executive Engineer Zila Parishad, Solapur.

2. Terms and Conditions
1) The Nimbargi Gram Panchayat will be the operational mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the approval of the Administrator.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
3. Under Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0, Nimbargi Gram Panchayat has received a prize of Rs. The first instalment of the prize money of ₹7.5 lakh each has been distributed to them. As the Government has accorded administrative sanction for the Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken out of the said prize money as per paragraph-1 above, the Government is approving the release of the second instalment of the remaining amount of the prize money to Nimbargi Gram Panchayat amounting to Rs. 7.5 lakh (Rupees Seven Lakh Fifty Thousand only) through Maharashtra Pollution Control Board.
