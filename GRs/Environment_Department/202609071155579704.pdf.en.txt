The Proposal:
The procedure for the award of cash prizes to the winning local bodies in the 12 categories of Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 and the appropriation of the said prize money has been notified vide Government Decision dated 27.09.2024 at Reference No. (1). Accordingly, a Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken up by the local bodies out of the award amount has been prepared vide Government Decision dated 08.02.2022 at Reference No. Administrative approval has to be obtained from a committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change.
2. Accordingly, for grant of administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report received from the Awardee Local Bodies, Ms. The Committee has made recommendations after discussing the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Local Bodies in its meeting held on 07.04.2026 under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change. It was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report of the respective local bodies as recommended by the committee.
The government's decision:
Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of the works to be taken up out of the total prize money of Rs.15,00 / - (Rupees Fifteen Lakhs Seventy-nine Only) received by the Gram Panchayat under Maji Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 has been submitted by the Village Development Officer, TARAS Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of the Executive Engineer, Zila Parishad Thane. The proposal was submitted by the Vasind Gram Panchayat. Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change

2. Terms and Conditions
1) Basind Gram Panchayat will be the operational mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the approval of the Administrator.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
3. Under Majhi Basundhara Abhiyan 4.0, Vasind Gram Panchayat has received the prize money of Rs. 15.00 lakhs for the second position at the divisional level in the "Gram Panchayat Group (population more than 10 thousand)" category out of which 50% i. e. Rs. 7.5 lakhs has been distributed to them. Since the detailed project report of the works to be taken out of this prize money has been granted administrative sanction by the Government as per paragraphs 1 and 2 above, the Government is approving the disbursement of the second instalment of the remaining prize money of Rs. 7.5 lakh (Rs. Seven lakh Fifty thousand only) to the AS Gram Panchayat through the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board.
