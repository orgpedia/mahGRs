The procedure for the award of cash prizes to the winning local bodies in the 12 categories of the award has been notified vide Government Decision dated 27.09.2024 at Reference No. (1). Accordingly, a Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken up by the local bodies out of the award amount has been prepared vide Government Decision dated 08.02.2022 at Reference No. Administrative approval has to be obtained from a committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change.
2. Accordingly, for grant of administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report received from the Awardee Local Bodies, Ms. The Committee has made recommendations after discussing the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Local Bodies in its meeting held on 07.04.2026 under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change. It was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report of the respective local bodies as recommended by the committee.
Government Decision: Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, a detailed project report of the total amount of Rs. 15.00 lacs to be received by Materbadi Gram Panchayat under Magee Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 was submitted by Village Development Officer, Materbadi Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of Executive Engineer, Jalna Zilla Parishad for the cost of Rs. 14,98, 848.00 (Rupees Four Hundred and Eighty Four Lakhs AGAMA Thousand Eight Hundred and Forty Eight Only). As per the recommendation made by the said Committee, the works in the Detailed Project Report submitted by Mhatrewadi Gram Panchayat at a cost of Rs.14,98, 848.00 (Rupees Fourteen Lakh Ninety Eight Thousand Eight Hundred and Forty Eight only) are being accorded administrative sanction by the Government under the following conditions: The works for which administrative sanction is being granted are shown in the accompanying Annexure-9.
2. Terms and Conditions 1) The implementation mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report shall be the Materbadi Gram Panchayat.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the date of administrative approval.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
3. Under Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0, Mhatrewadi Gram Panchayat was awarded the third position at the divisional level in the "Gram Panchayat Group (population less than 1.50 thousand)" category at a cost of Rs. A prize of ₹1 lakh has been received.
Out of this, 50 per cent i. e. Rs. 7.5 lakh has been disbursed to them.
