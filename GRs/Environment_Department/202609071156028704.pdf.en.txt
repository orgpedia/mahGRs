The procedure for the award of cash prizes to the winning local bodies in the 12 categories of the award has been notified vide Government Decision dated 27.09.2024 at Reference No. (1). Accordingly, a Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken up by the local bodies out of the award amount has been prepared vide Government Decision dated 08.02.2022 at Reference No. Administrative approval has to be obtained from a committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Environment F Climate Change Department.
Accordingly, for administrative approval of the Detailed Project Report received from the Awardee Local Bodies, Ms. The Committee has made recommendations after discussing the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Local Bodies in its meeting held on 07.04.2026 under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change. It was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report of the respective local bodies as recommended by the committee.
Government Decision: Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of the works to be taken up out of the total prize money of Rs.15,000 / - (Rupees Fifteen Lakh only) to be received by Chandori Gram Panchayat under Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 has been submitted by Village Development Officer, Chandori Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of Executive Engineer, Zila Parishad Nashik. The proposal was submitted by Chandori Gram Panchayat. The Committee constituted under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change held its meetings on 07.04.2026.

2. Terms and Conditions:
1) Chandori Gram Panchayat will be the operational mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the date of administrative approval.
4) If the amount of the prize exceeds the amount, it should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
