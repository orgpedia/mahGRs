The government's decision:
Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of the total prize money of Rs.499.99 lakh (Rupees Ninety-Nine Lakh AAMT Thousand Eight Hundred and Twenty-Six Only) to be received by the Red Gram Panchayat under Maji Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 has been submitted by the Village Development Officer, Red Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of the Executive Engineer Zila Parishad, Raigad. The proposal was submitted by the Tambati Gram Panchayat. The date of the committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change was taken into consideration in its meeting held on 07.04.2026. As per the recommendation made by the said Committee, the Government is giving administrative sanction to the works in the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Tambati Gram Panchayat at a cost of Rs. 49,99, 826.00 (Rupees Ninety-nine Lakh Ninety-Nine Thousand Eight Hundred and Twenty-Six Only) subject to the following conditions and conditions. The works for which administrative sanction is being granted are shown in the accompanying Annexure-9.
2. Terms and Conditions
1) The operational mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report shall be the Red Gram Panchayat.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the approval of the Administrator.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
