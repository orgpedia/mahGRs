Preamble:
Consequent upon the insertion of Section 21A in the Air Act, 1981 and Section 27A in the
Water Act, 1974 by the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023, the Ministry of
Environment, Forest and Climate Change issued the Control of Air Pollution and Control of Water
Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent) Guidelines, 2025, which were thereafter
amended by the Control of Air Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent) Amendment
Guidelines, 2026 and the Control of Water Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent)
Amendment Guidelines, 2026 (in force from 27.01.2026). Every State Board 1s required to act in
accordance with the Guidelines as so amended.
Accordingly, Government of Maharashtra issued Government Resolution No.: ENV-
2026/CR-158/TC-1, dated 25.08.2026 regarding the Consent Fee calculations. Consequent upon
the issue of Government Resolution dated 25.08.2026, the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board,
vide their letter No AS(T)/2026/21, dated 07.09.2026 sent the proposal for suggesting certain
amendments.
The amendments suggested by the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board have been
considered by Government and the following decision has been taken by Government of
Maharashtra in this regard.
GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION:
In exercise of the power conferred by the Control of Air Pollution and Control of Water
Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent) Guidelines, 2025, which were thereafter
amended by the Control of Air Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent) Amendment
Guidelines, 2026 and the Control of Water Pollution (Grant, Refusal or Cancellation of Consent)
Amendment Guidelines, 2026, the amendments in the Government Resolution dated 25.08.2026
as follows;
