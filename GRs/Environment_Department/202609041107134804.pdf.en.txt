Government Decision: Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report worth Rs.15,00 / - (Rupees Fifteen Lakh Four Hundred and Five Only) out of the total prize money of Rs.15,00 / - (Rupees Fifteen Lakh Four Hundred and Five Only) received by Savkhed Khandala Gram Panchayat under Majhi Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0 has been submitted by Village Development Officer, Savkhed Khandala Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of Executive Engineer, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Zilla Parishad. The proposal was submitted by Savkhed Khandala Gram Panchayat.
The Committee constituted under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change was considered in its meetings held on 07.04.2026. As per the recommendation made by the said Committee, the works in the Detailed Project Report submitted by Savkhed Khandala Gram Panchayat at a cost of Rs.15,00, 405.00 (Rupees Fifteen Lakh Four Hundred and Five only) are being accorded administrative sanction by the Government subject to the following conditions and conditions. The works for which administrative approval is being granted are shown in the accompanying Annexure-9.
2. Terms and Conditions
1) Savkhed Khandala Gram Panchayat will be the operational mechanism for the works in this Detailed Project Report.
2) The Financial F Physical Progress Report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the approval of the Administrator.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
