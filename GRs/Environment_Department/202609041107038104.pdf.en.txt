Preamble: My TET Mission 4. Cash prizes to the winning local bodies in the 12 categories has been announced under the Government Decision dated 27th September, 2024 at Reference No. (1). Accordingly, a Detailed Project Report of the works to be taken up by the local bodies out of the award amount has been prepared vide Government Decision dated 08.02.2022 at Reference No. Administrative approval has to be obtained from a committee constituted under the chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change.
2. Accordingly, for grant of administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report received from the Awardee Local Bodies, Ms. The Committee has made recommendations after discussing the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Local Bodies in its meetings held on 07.04.2026 under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment & Climate Change. It was under the consideration of the government to grant administrative approval to the Detailed Project Report of the respective local bodies as recommended by the committee.
Government Decision: Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of the works to be taken up out of the total prize money of Rs. 50.00 lakh to be received by the Mandrup Gram Panchayat under MARI ACT Mission 4.0, costing Rs. 50,02, 351.00 (Rupees fifty lakhs two thousand three hundred and fifty one only) was submitted by the Village Development Officer, AST Gram Panchayat to the Executive Engineer, Zila Parishad Solapur.

As per the recommendation made by the said Committee, the works in the Detailed Project Report submitted by the Mandrup Gram Panchayat at a total cost of Rs.50,02, 351.00 (Rupees Fifty Lakh Two Thousand Three Hundred and Fifty Only) are being accorded administrative sanction by the Government subject to the following conditions and conditions. The works for which administrative sanction is being granted are shown in the accompanying Annexure-19.
2. Terms and Conditions 1) The HST Gram Panchayat shall be the implementing agency for the works in this Detailed Project Report.
2) The financial and physical progress report of the implementation of the works mentioned in this Detailed Project Report should be sent to my Vasundhara Mission Directorate by the 5th of every month.
3) The implementation of the works in this Detailed Project Report should be completed within the next 6 months from the approval of the Administrator.
4) Expenditure in excess of the prize money should be made out of self-funding.
5) In case of any increase in the original project cost in this Detailed Project Report for any reason, no additional funds / grants will be made available for the same.
3. Mandrup Gram Panchayat has received a prize of Rs. 50.00 lakhs for standing first at the block level in the "Gram Panchayat Group (population more than 10 thousand)" category in My Vasundhara Abhiyan 4.0. Out of this, 50 per cent i. e. Rs. 25.00 lakh has been disbursed to them. Since the detailed project report of the works to be taken out of this prize money has been granted administrative approval by the Government as per paragraphs 1 and 2 above, the Government is approving the distribution of the remaining amount of the prize money to the Mandrup Gram Panchayat through the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) in the second instalment of Rs.
