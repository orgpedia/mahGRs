Government Decision: Considering the points mentioned in the proposal, the detailed project report of the total prize money of Rs. 75.00 lakh to be received by the village panchayat under Maji Vasundhara Abhiyaan 3. 0 has been submitted by the Village Development Officer, Jarud Gram Panchayat with the technical approval of the Executive Engineer Zilla Parishad, Amravati. The proposal was submitted by the HSS Gram Panchayat. The Committee constituted under the Chairmanship of Secretary, Department of Environment and Climate Change was considered in its meetings held on 07.04.2026. As per the recommendation made by the said Committee, the works in the Detailed Project Report submitted by Jarud Gram Panchayat at a cost of Rs. 75,00, 000.00 (Rs. Seventy Five Lakhs HAT) are being accorded administrative sanction by the Government under the following conditions and MAA. The works for which administrative sanction is being granted are shown in the accompanying Annexure-9.
