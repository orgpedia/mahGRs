Introduction: As per the notification dated 06.12.2023 of the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions (Department of Personnel and Training), Government of India, the strength of the cadre posts in the Indian Administrative Service in the state has been fixed. Accordingly, 8 cadre posts are sanctioned in Apex Scale: Level 17 in the Pay Matrix. Also, as per the provision in Rule 12 (7) of the Pay Scale Rules, 2016, 8 more Non-Cadre Posts (SDRs) can be filled. 7 temporary posts have been created in the Apex Scale: Level 17 in the Pay Matrix category by a Government decision under the above reference.
At present, the government was contemplating the creation of another temporary post in the cadre in view of the administrative exigencies.
The Government's Decisions:
Under the second proviso to Rule 4 (2) of the Indian Administrative Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, the Government is approving the creation of 01 AGM post in the cadre Apex Scale: Level 17 in the Pay Matrix tentatively from the date 01.09.2026 to the date 31.12.2026.
The Government decision was made available on the Government of Maharashtra website www.maharashtra.gov.in.
