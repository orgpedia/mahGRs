The Proposal:
The Ministry under the Department of General Administration deals with the various subjects of Dining Halls / Restaurants. A review meeting was held at Vidhan Bhavan on 12/03/2026 under the chairmanship of the Minister (Marketing & Public Administration). In this meeting, modernization is being done to improve the quality of food in line with the catering service.
The issue came up. In it, the minister has suggested modernization to improve restaurant catering services.
Exhaust system has been installed in the kitchen of Mantralaya Restaurant B square restaurant.
But there is no system in place for the outside air to come in or for the cold air to come in. Food safety, hygiene, and employee safety are all important aspects of the kitchen.
For this, the kitchen needs to be modernized. Technical manpower is not available in line with the modernization of the Ministry Restaurant B square restaurant. For this, Dr. Prashant Virkhare, Head of Department (Tourism), Government Technological University, Nagpur, was appointed as the Technical Advisor.
Mr. Virkhare, on behalf of MAHA Technological Institute, Nagpur, has inspected both the above mentioned restaurants and submitted his report dated 18/6/2026. The report includes structural repairs, flooring, wall tiles, and waterproofing of clean and washable surfaces under civil renovation. Replacement / Up-gradation of appliances in line with kitchen appliances - including stainless steel, modular and energy-efficient appliances. It is recommended to install mechanical exhaust systems and HVAC to reduce heat, humidity and fumes in line with ventilation, HVAC, measures to ensure adequate sinks and sanitizers for washing utensils, and adherence to fly nets, hand wash centres and uniform / hygiene standards.
