The Proposal:
There is a need for a thorough review of the existing examination system with a view to making the existing recruitment examinations conducted by the state government more transparent, safe, reliable, candidate-oriented and to prevent malpractices and unfair means. This requires an in-depth study of the entire examination process, including preparation and examination of question papers, confidentiality and safe handling, management and security of examination centres, use of technology, prevention of malpractices, coordination among various agencies, grievance redressal and overall examination-experience of the candidates. In a meeting held on 12 August 2026 under the chairmanship of the Chief Minister, it was decided to constitute a high-level committee to suggest comprehensive reforms in the recruitment examinations in the state by thoroughly reviewing the existing examination systems.
The government's decision:
The government is setting up a high-level committee to conduct a detailed study of the recruitment examination system in Maharashtra and suggest necessary reforms as follows:

2. Terms of Reference:
To review the existing system and procedures of recruitment examinations to be conducted by various departments, authorities and examination conducting bodies of the State Government.
Suggest comprehensive measures to make the 2.भरत exam more secure, transparent, reliable, impartial, and widely accepted by candidates and relevant stakeholders.
3. To suggest appropriate safeguards by studying all processes like preparation of question papers, scrutiny / moderation, preparation of more than one questionnaire, print or digital distribution, confidentiality, custody, transportation of question papers, conduct of examinations, response registration, evaluation and declaration of results.
4. Make recommendations on the effective use of information technology, digital systems and security technologies in the entire examination process, as well as necessary authentication, access control, audit trails, monitoring and other technology-based security measures.
5. Review and suggest preventive, supervisory and accountability measures to prevent and detect leakage of question papers, breach of confidentiality of question papers, tampering of candidates, consensual use of prohibited electronic devices, and other malpractices.
To study the relevant best practices followed in the 6.इतर states as well as the examination systems in other countries as required.
7. To suggest minimum standards for selection of examination centres, infrastructure, monitoring, safety, manpower and work readiness.
8. Suggest institutional mechanisms for effective coordination among District Collectors, Commissioners of Police / Superintendents of Police, authorities in the education sector, examination bodies and other relevant agencies.
9. To visit every revenue division in the state and discuss, consult, suggest and work methods with students / candidates, representatives of educational institutions and other relevant stakeholders, taking into consideration their views and suggestions.
10. Suggest measures for effective grievance redressal, interaction with candidates as well as strengthening the confidence of candidates in the fairness and integrity of the examination process.

The High-Level Committee shall receive suggestions and feedback in accordance with the above mentioned issues by conducting a detailed study and interaction / consultation with the concerned stakeholders, either in person or online, and submit a comprehensive final report to the Government.
