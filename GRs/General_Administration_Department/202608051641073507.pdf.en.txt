The appointment by the deputation shall initially be for a period of three years, after which the period may be extended for a maximum period of five years with the consent of the officer concerned and the Cadre Controlling Authority serving as the deputation, taking into account the requirements.
3. All Administrative Departments are requested to bring this circular to the notice of all officers of the above mentioned cadre working under their jurisdiction.
4. The officer / employee desirous and eligible for appointment by the deputation to the post shall forward their application in the prescribed format enclosed with it to the institution through their administrative department with a plan to reach the institution by the date 25/08/2026.

5. Officers / employees seeking appointment by deputation should enclose with the application a certificate from the Head of the Office of the Commissioner regarding their current service in the administration.
6. Government Decisions, General Administration Department (Task-12), Govt. 17.12.2016 and serial number d. 16.2.2018 and SAPRAV / (Task-12A), d. Appointment by deputation shall be scrutinized in accordance with the provisions, terms and conditions laid down in the 11.5.2015.
7. 8. After scrutinizing the applications received on the last date, proposals regarding eligible applications will be forwarded to the General Administration Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai for further action.
