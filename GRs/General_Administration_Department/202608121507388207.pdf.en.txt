the Proposal.
The Maharashtra State Information Commission has been set up under Section 15 of the Right to Information Act, 2005. Section 15 (2) (b) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 provides that the State Information Commission shall consist of a Chief Information Commissioner and not more than 10 State Information Commissioners.
The order dated 10th February, 2026 in the 4236/2018 directs the creation of three additional posts of State Information Commissioners in the state of Maharashtra and immediate commencement of the process of appointment thereon.
With a view to implement the said orders of the Supreme Court of India and to dispose of the pending second appeals in the State Information Commission more effectively, three additional posts of State Information Commissioners as well as 15 posts of supporting officers and staff required for them are to be created. It has been approved by the state cabinet, which met on August 04, 2026. Accordingly, the following Government Decisions are being issued.
The Government's Decisions:
Considering the number of pending second appeals from the State Information Commission, the Government is approving the creation of the following new posts for appointment of Additional State Information Commissioners and such officers / staff as may be required for them for a period of 3 years on temporary basis in the Benches of Brihanmumbai, Nashik and Amravati.

The State Chief Information Commissioner shall have the power to appoint State Information Commissioners to other Benches or transfer to them certain second appeals from other Benches, as may be necessary, if the number of pending appeals in the said Bench is reduced.
3. Out of the above posts, the posts of Deputy Secretary and Room Officer shall be filled by deputation by the Government. The State Information Commission shall have the power to fill the posts of short-list, clerk-typician and constable on contractual basis through an external mechanism. The State Information Commission should follow the guidelines in the Government Circular dated 27.04.2022 of the Department of Finance while taking up the services of TASTIER posts.
4. The estimated recurring cost of creation of the post is about Rs. 4. 23 crores per annum and non-recurring expenditure of Rs. 75.00 will remain as BA.
