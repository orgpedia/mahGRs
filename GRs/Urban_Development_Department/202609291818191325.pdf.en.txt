The Proposal: -
Under section 147-A (9) of the Maharashtra Municipal Councils and Industrial Townships Act 1965, in the case of articles in a municipal area, in the case of sale or donation of property so situated in a municipal area, a surcharge of 1 per cent is levied on the value of the property and, in the case of a consumable mortgage account, on the amount credited by that article, as specified in that article, and subsection (3) provides for the disbursement to the respective municipal councils of an amount equal to the amount of additional duty charged as such surcharge.
