Preamble: - Hinduhriday Samrat Balasaheb Thackeray Maharashtra Samriddhi Highway, the state's ambitious project connecting the state capital Mumbai and the sub-capital Nagpur, has been opened for traffic. Nagpur is a major centre for education, IT, health and industry-related services for the population living within a 500 km radius, including the adjoining states of Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Andhra Pradesh.
Nagpur is the sub-capital city of the state of Maharashtra and the third-largest city in the state. Nagpur is the thirteenth-largest urban area in India. Nagpur is geographically located in the central part of the country. Nagpur is known as the Orange City as it has a large orange market. The International Logistic Park is an important facility for the transportation and storage of goods in Nagpur, greatly benefiting the agricultural and mineral businesses and industries of Nagpur and the area.
The Hindu Hriday Samrat Balasaheb Thackeray Maharashtra Samriddhi Highway is planned to be extended from Nagpur to Gondia with a view to connecting the state of Maharashtra with the Western Border Shipping Expressway. Nagpur-Gondiya district has huge natural resources including minerals, agricultural products. This rapid AMMEN ANCA will provide a boost to the rural economy as it will be connected to the mineral and Naxalite affected areas as well as major taluka locations. The Nagpur-Gondia Access Controlled Expressway, planned for the "holistic development" of the natural mineral-rich Least Developed Areas of Nagpur, Gondia, Gadchiroli and Chandrapur districts in the Eastern Division of Maharashtra State, will help in the socio-economic development of the tribal areas by connecting them to the mainstream, promoting new industries and generating large-scale employment.
The 162.577 km of Nagpur-Gondia Access Controlled Expressway has been closed as per the Government Decision dated 03.10.2025. M.Lambis has been approved. The land acquisition amount for the project is also Rs. 2, 006.03 crore (principal) and potential interest of Rs. 1, 156.16 crore (interest) totalling to Rs. 3, 162.18 crores and the project has been approved to be taken up by the Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation.
