Introduction: The existing road network for the city of Pune is now beginning to become inadequate due to the industrial progress that has taken place around the city of Pune. Also, Pune city is growing rapidly in urbanization with Pune city leading the country in education sector, IT and auto industry.
The proposed Pune Ring Road will intersect three important national highways passing through the city of Pune, namely, Mumbai-Rebaglur, Mumbai-Hyderabad-Vijayawada, Pune-Nashik, and the new national highways Pune-Pandharpur, Pune-Chatrapati Sambhajinagar, Pune-Mangaon, and Pune Mumbai Expressway. All types of inter-state transport as well as intra-state transport are currently passing through the inner parts of the city as there is no external circular route.
This has led to internal traffic problems, adding to traffic congestion and pollution. As a solution to this, the Public Works Department has proposed to construct an outer ring road around the city of Pune. The Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation has been declared as the implementing agency under the Government Decision dated 94.07.2015.
The Cabinet Committee on Infrastructure (CCI) As per the decision of the meeting held on 25.08.2021, 4 sections of the ring road around Pune city will be given. Administrative approval was granted by the Government Decision of 06.09.2021.
