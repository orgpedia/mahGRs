The Proposal: -
The state's ambitious project connecting the state capital Mumbai and the sub-capital Nagpur has been opened for traffic on the Hindu Hriday Samrat Balasaheb Thackeray Maharashtra Samriddhi Highway. Nagpur is a major centre for education, IT, health and industry-related services for the population living within a 500 km radius, including the adjoining states of Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Andhra Pradesh.
Nagpur is the sub-capital city of the state of Maharashtra and the third-largest city in the state. Nagpur is the thirteenth-largest urban area in India. Nagpur is geographically located in the central part of the country. Nagpur is known as the Orange City as it has a large orange market. The International Logistic Park is an important facility for the transportation and storage of goods in Nagpur, greatly benefiting agro-mineral businesses and industries in Nagpur and the area.
The Hindu Hriday Samrat Balasaheb Thackeray Maharashtra Samriddhi Highway is planned to be extended to Nagpur-Chandrapur with a view to connecting the state of Maharashtra with the Western Border Shipping Expressway. Nagpur-Gondiya district has huge natural resources including minerals, agricultural products. The rural economy will get a boost as the expressway will connect tribal, mineral and Naxal-affected areas as well as major taluka locations. The Nagpur-Chandrapur Access Controlled Expressway, planned for the "holistic development" of the natural mineral-rich Least Developed Areas of Nagpur, Gondia, Gadchiroli and Chandrapur districts in the Eastern Division of Maharashtra State, will help in the socio-economic development of the tribal areas by connecting them to the mainstream, promoting new industries and generating large-scale employment.
As per the government decision dated December 1, the Nagpur-Chandrapur Access Controlled Expressway will have a length of 204.799 km. A revised design of m length has been approved. The land acquisition amount for the project is also Rs. 1, 493.31 crore (principal) and potential interest of Rs. 860.63 crore (interest)
