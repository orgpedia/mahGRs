Preamble: - National Highway No. The route from Old Pune Naka to Boramani Naka, passing through Solapur city above 65, is very important for the city as well as the interstate flow and is causing heavy traffic jams on this route. As a result, problems such as vehicle delays, increased risk of accidents, increased pollution, and adverse impacts on the city's transport system continue to arise.
In order to greatly reduce traffic congestion in Solapur city by creating seamless connectivity in the major internal roads of Solapur city and to promote safe, fast and easy commute of citizens by promoting industrial, commercial and social development, the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) proposed two flyovers in Solapur city on the above traffic problem on 03rd July, 2026. The meeting with all the concerned agencies was concluded under the chairmanship of the Chief Minister, State of Maharashtra. The meetings were held on National Highway No. The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) has issued a notification to declare over 65 flyovers as Government approved Vital Public Projects and as a permanent solution to the traffic problem. Discussions were held on developing a four-lane elevated corridor between Old Pune Naka at 65 and Boramani Naka of about 9.660 km length as well as approach ramps and service roads where required.
