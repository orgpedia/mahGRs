1. Preamble
The Government of Maharashtra is at progressive state and transforming with large
quality development project in infrastructure. The object of this endeavour is committed
to ensuring quality, timely completion, transparency and accountability in execution of
public infrastructure projects.
The existing procurement framework evaluates the technical and financial capacity of
bidders based on the prevalent criteria. However, non performing contractors or
underperforming firms participating in infrastructure projects challenging the Government
in forms of inadequate manpower, equipment or financial capacity leading to missed
milestones and prolonged completion time, undue increase in public inconvenience,
postpone economic benefits, cost overruns, poor quality of construction, increased
litigation and disputes. In view of the above, detailed verification of actual performance
of contractors in terms of quality of construction, timely completion, safety, contractual
compliance and overall project management is necessary, in order to identify and
discourage the non-performing contractors from seeking further works.
The Government of Maharashtra considers it necessary to supplement the existing
qualification criteria with a structured, evidence-based assessment of actual contractor
performance across the project life-cycle planning, mobilisation, quality assurance,
safety, contractual compliance, timely completion and maintenance. Such a mechanism
will progressively reward consistent excellence, encourage improvement among weaker
performers, reduce subjective disputes and improve value for public money.
In order to identify the non-performing contractors, Government of Maharashtra has
decided to introduce a “Contractor Performance Assessment and Rating

authorities.
3. Tender Clause
The following clause shall be mandatorily introduced in all the tender documents for
projects of Rs.500 Crores or more:
Clause: Performance Certificate for Ongoing Projects
The Bidder shall submit, as part of its tender documents, a valid Performance Certificate,
issued by an officer not below the rank of Chief Engineer (or equivalent), of each

Statutory Department / Government Instrumentality where the Bidder has or had an
ongoing or completed project.
The Performance Certificate shall specifically evaluate and certify the following
parameters in respect of the Bidder's performance on the relevant project:
(1) Number of delays, their nature, and extent;
(ii) Quality of workmanship and compliance with quality control norms; and
(iii) Overall Performance.
The Performance Certificate shall remain valid for a period of Twelve (12) months from
the date of its issuance. A certificate beyond this validity period shall not be accepted.
Projects claimed in technical capacity or similar project without a valid Performance
Certificate shall not be considered for evaluation. Also, ongoing projects reflected in
"Work in Hand’ for calculation of Bid Capacity without a valid performance certificate,
will lead to consideration of twice the awarded value of that work in 'Value of B’.
4. Performance Certificate
4.1 Every bidder claiming Technical Capacity or similar project shall submit a valid
Performance Certificate for each eligible project relied upon for qualification and for
every ongoing project reflected in 'Value of B' for calculation of Bid Capacity.
4.2 The Performance Certificate shall be issued only in the format prescribed under this
resolution.
4.3 The certificate shall be issued by an officer not below the rank of Chief Engineer or
equivalent.
4.4 The Performance Certificate shall remain valid for twelve months from the date of
issue.
4.5 Experience claimed without a valid Performance Certificate shall not be considered
for Technical Capacity evaluation. And ongoing projects reflected in "Work in Hand’ for
calculation of Bid Capacity without a valid performance certificate, will lead to
consideration of twice the awarded value of that work in 'Value of B’.
4.6 The Performance Certificate shall contain, inter alia, the assessment. of:
- Timely completion (this will be assessed differently for completed and ongoing works);
- Quality of work;
- Overall project performance.

5. Contractor Rating
5.1 Contractor Rating shall be calculated by Bid Inviting Authority based upon the
weighted average of all eligible Performance Certificates submitted by the Contractor.
5.2 The weightage considered shall be based on cost of work completed excluding
variations, escalations and taxes.

7. Joint Ventures
Where a project has been executed through a Joint Venture, the Performance Certificate
shall clearly specify the scope and amount of work executed by each JV Partner. As far
as practicable, Performance Rating shall be attributed only to the JV Partner responsible
for execution of the relevant works.
