Ganeshotsav on the occasion of upcoming Ganeshotsav
On providing toll waiver and other facilities to Ganesh devotees visiting Konkan during the period.
Government of Maharashtra
Department of Public Works,
Circular No. Khaksas-2024 / P.No.329 / Roads-8
Mantralaya, Mumbai - Yoo 032.
Dated 8th September 2026
Circular
The government was considering providing tax exemption facilities to vehicles of Ganesh devotees going to Konkan during the upcoming Ganeshotsav period of 2026.
Accordingly, the government has taken the following decision.
1. D. 11.09.2026 that is. During this period, vehicles of Ganeshotsav devotees going to Konkan and Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation buses are being exempted from tax at the toll plazas on Mumbai-Bengaluru National Highway (Ram-48), Mumbai-Goa National Highway (Ram-66), Public Works Department and Konkan-bound roads and Urban Development Department under Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation.
A) For this purpose, stickers in the form of "Ganeshotsav 2026 Konkan Darshan" stickers with the text "Tax-free pass" with the text "Train number, Driver's name," as attached, should be made available to Police Stations, Traffic Police SMB RTO Offices in coordination with Transport Department, Traffic Police / Police, concerned Regional Transport Department (RTO) as per the number required.
Passes will also be accepted for return travel.
b) The district from where the Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation buses will come for Ganeshotsav. Passes will be made available to the police department or regional transport department there, as in the above vat. Maharashtra State Transport Corporation buses departing for Gauri-Ganapati traffic from the respective district will be charged / pasted with a team tax-exempt pass.
The consolidated information regarding the number of passes to be issued by the rural or urban police / Regional Transport Office should be submitted to the Deputy Secretary (Kh1), Public Works Department, 4th Floor, Mantralaya, Mumbai-32 for information.

3. The Police and Transport Department should publish the information about the pass facilities and their availability during Ganeshotsav in the notification / advertisement as required for the public.
The aforesaid instructions should be brought to the notice of all concerned authorities.
