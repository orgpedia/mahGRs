Against the above-mentioned engineers, the orders of promotion are being issued under Rule 8B 10 of the Maharashtra Civil Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1979 subject to the condition that the divisional post is in operation or the proposed / court case is not in operation. However, these orders will automatically stand cancelled in case of any of these engineers against whom a Divisional Magistrate / Court case is pending on the date of this order.
4. The concerned Chief Engineer, Public Works Regional Department is requested to relieve the Junior Engineer (Electrical) (Diploma holder) appointed to them with immediate effect as per the said promotion order and to join the new place of appointment without going on leave with minimum tenure.
5. The Junior Engineer (Electrical) (Diploma holder) concerned shall join the post as promoted within 9 months from the date of this order, otherwise the General Administration Department may notify the same. As per the 14/07/2021, the period of absence of the concerned Junior Engineer (Electrical) (Diploma holder) will be treated as a non-deceiving day. Also, their attention is being drawn to Rule-23 of the Maharashtra Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1979. In view of the provisions of this rule, they will be liable for disciplinary action if they submit any form of application for change of office.
