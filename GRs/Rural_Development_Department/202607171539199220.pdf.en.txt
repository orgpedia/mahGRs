Introduction -
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana is a scheme launched by Government
of India for connectivity of Rural Habitations of + 500 populations (+250
populations of schedule-V areas). The Bridge Project Proposals under Pradhan
Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana-III was submitted for approval of Empowered
Committee of Ministry of Rural Development, New Delhi. It was discussed in the
Empowered Committee and the Empowered Committee has given approval to the
Bridge Project Proposals. Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India
accorded approval for the proposals of Bridges vide letter No.P-17024/15/2020-RC
(371917), Dated 13 July,2026 with various condition.

The Bridge Project Proposals under Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana-III
