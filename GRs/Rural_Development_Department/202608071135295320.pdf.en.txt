The Proposal: -
In order to encourage and enhance the efficiency of Gram Panchayats, which are predominantly functioning as local self-government institutions in rural areas, by encouraging them to perform, Reference No.1 for implementation of "Mukhyamantri Samriddhi Panchayati Raj Abhiyan" in the State from this financial year for Gram Panchayats, Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads doing excellent work. Approval has been granted by a Government Decision of 1.
Under this mission, the taluk-level and district-level evaluation process is in full swing and the division-level evaluation process is underway. Reference No. 3 As per the Government decision here, the top two best Gram Panchayats selected at the divisional level will be evaluated by the State Level Committee and the top three scoring Gram Panchayats will be selected as the best Gram Panchayats at the state level. Accordingly, Reference No. 1 The Government was considering the formation of a revised State Level Evaluation Committee by modifying the State Level Evaluation Committee constituted under the Government Decision here.
The government's decision:
For State Level Assessment under Mukhyamantri Samriddhi Panchayati Raj Abhiyan, Shri. A revised State Level Evaluation Committee is being constituted under the Chairmanship of Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary (Rural Development & Panchayati Raj) as under:
