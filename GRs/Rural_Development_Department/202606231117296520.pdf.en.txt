Introduction -
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana is launched by Government of India for
connectivity of rural habitations, in the year 2000. Accordingly, PMGSY-IV has been
approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2024 and is to be implemented for the
Period 2024-25 to 2028 - 29.Under This phase habitats with population 500 + in
general areas, 250+ in tribal / aspirational blocks and 100+ in LWE (Naxal-affected)
areas will be connected by all-weather roads. The Project Proposal for road works
under Batch-I of PMGSY-IV was submitted for approval of Empowered Committee,
Ministry Of Rural Development, Government of India. It was discussed in the
Empowered Committee and the Empowered Committee has given approval to the
Project Proposals. Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India accorded
approval for the proposals of roads vide letter No.P-17024/16/2025-RC(393336),
Dated 18 May, 2026 with various condition.

2. The above clearance is accorded subject to the following conditions to be fulfilled by the State:-
(i) Quality Control labs must compulsorily be established and geo-tagged time- stamped, photograph of Engineer-in-Charge visit in the lab should be uploaded on OMMAS before commencement of the work.
(ii) State will upload Design Stage Road Safety Audit reports of all the roads proposed on OMMAS before tendering.
(iii) State must ensure that the payment to Cement Concrete roads is made only after verification of target strength achieved after 28 days based on cores taken from the pavement as per MoRD specifications.
(iv) In case of Panelled concrete roads, State shall ensure that initial dummy joint cutting of concrete is done to their required depth within the stipulated time limit of 24-48 hours and as per relevant codes and sound engineering practice to avoid development of haphazard cracks in the concrete. For this the state shall upload the Geo-tagged and time- stamped photographs, video recording both during laying and joint cutting of concrete. State is also advised to adhere the directions mentioned in circular issued vide letter No. NRRDA- PO13(11)/1/2020-Dir(P-I) dated 11th April, 2023.
