Introduction:-
The project proposal of Phase X has been cleared by Ministry of Rural
Development, Government of India vide letter dated 14th August, 2012.
Accordingly the Government of Maharashtra has accorded Administrative
Approval to the project proposal for 452 missing bridges on already sanctioned
roads and 9 road works.
In this regard it is to state that, Repackage No MH-1563 Construction of
Major bridge SH-111 Soshtevadi Fata to Kumbhawade Gavadi Road at Ch. 0/125
Km. of Tal.Shahuwadi, District-Kolhapur 1s sanctioned under Phase X, Batch-lI.
As per the proposal submitted by Chief Engineer, MRRDA it 15 to state that the
bridge in km 0/125 on Kasari River @ SH-111 Soshtevadi Fata to Kumbhawade
Gavadi road is approved with 3 spans of 15.00m C/c with foundation in M-25
grade of concrete pier, pier footing & cap. As per the previously approved GAD,
it was proposed to construct High level Bridge for a length of 45m.

During execution it is noticed that due to protest from the villagers, the work was
unable to start at the location as per the approved GAD. Therefore on insepection
and verification by Superintending Engineer (BR.), Designs Circle, Konkan
Bhavan, Navi Mumbai during visit for approval of founadation, it 15 decided to
shift the alignment of the proposed site about 60 m on U/s of existing KT weir.
Hence there will be significant variation in bridge length as per revised cross
section of the river. It 15 proposed to construct 2 lane submersible bridge, which
consists 3 spans of 18.00m C/c_ having total length of 54 m clearing HFL with
minimum vertical clearance of 0.3 m. Therefore to reduce length of the bridge , it
15 proposed to construct two lane submersible bridge. Due to this the actual
expenditure against the Gol revised sanctioned cost 1s increased from Rs.183.73
lakhs to Rs.364.19 lakhs for construction of Submersible Bridge on Kasari River
@ SH-111 Soshtevadi Fata to Kumbhawade Gavadi road, at Ch 0/125, Tal.
Shahuwadi of Kolhapur District. As per the guidelines of scheme the revised
proposal of scope and cost of this work of Kolhapur District was submitted for
approval to Ministry of Rural Development, New Delhi and the Ministry of Rural
Development, New Delhi has accorded approval for the proposal vide letter No.
D.O. No.- NRRDA-P014(12)/3/2018-JD (Tech), Dated 03.08.2020.
Resolution :
The revised scope of the bridge work of Repackage No MH-1563,
Construction of Major bridge SH-111 Soshtevadi Fata to Kumbhawade Gavadi
Road at Ch. 0/125 Km. of Tal.Shahuwadi, District-Kolhapur, Gol Cost Rs.183.73
Lakhs to Rs.364.19 Lakhs has been cleared by the Ministry of Rural Development,
Government of India vide letter No. D.O. No.- NRRDA-P014(12)/3/2018-JD
(Tech), Dated 03.08.2020.

Accordingly, the Government of Maharashtra is pleased to accord Revised
Administrative Approval for Revised scope and cost of above mentioned bridge
work sanctioned under Repackage No.MH-1563, from Rs.183.73 Lakhs to
Rs.364.19 Lakhs under Phase X Batch I, in Kolhapur district. As per the MoRD
letter dated 03.08.2020, MoRD and State share will be Rs.180.32 Lakhs and
Rs.183.87 Lakhs respectively. As per the MoRD letter dated 03.08.2020, MoRD
and State share will be borne and as per the revised cost. The additional excess
will be met by the State from savings of the same phase 1.e. Phase-X (2012-13)
under which the said road has been sanctioned. Accordingly appropriate changes in
OMMAS will be made with the help of C-DAC Pune.
