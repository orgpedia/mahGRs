Government Orders: -
The Maharashtra Special Public Safety Act, 2024 (General of 2025). 47) This date has come into effect from the date of its publication in the Government Gazette of Maharashtra on 15th December, 2025.
In exercise of the powers conferred by the provisions of sub-section (1) of section GAT of the said Act, the Government of Maharashtra hereby constitutes an Advisory Board for the purposes of this Act as follows: -
1 Mr. Ambadas Joshi, Retired Justice, Bombay High Court - Chairman 2 Mr. Nagesh V. Nhavkar, Retired Principal District and Sessions Judge - Member 32 Mr. Shishir Hire, Government SHIT Appellate Branch, Bombay High Court - Member
The Advisory Board shall function in accordance with the provisions of the Maharashtra Special Public Safety Act.
The term of office of Chairman B. Members of the said Advisory Board shall be for a period of 3 years from the date of publication of this Government Order.
This Government Order is available on the Government of Maharashtra website www.maharashtra.gov.in.
