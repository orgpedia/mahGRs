Introduction: The Central Government has implemented SNA-SPARSH (Just-in-Time) system for disbursement of funds under Jal Jeevan FART (JIM) 2.0, under which funds received from the Central Government will be disbursed directly through the e-Kuber system of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Under this new system, the Government of India provides for the provision of referral. The letter dated 27.02.2025 specifies that for distribution of funds in the coverage category, the central government's budget under Major Head 3601 specifies "35 - Subsidies for creation of capital gains * to be mapped to the object head PFMS system." According to the guidelines of the central government, it is technically necessary to have the "35-Object Head" available even in the respective headings of accounts at the state level to maintain a matching allocation of both the central FRM and state share funds.
Accordingly, under Jal Jeevan FR, a new target has been set for Central Share and State Share under General Component (Scope Category), namely, Account Head 2215 9791 (Central Share 50% - General Component) and Account Head 2215 2071 (State Share 50% - General Component), respectively, by way of figurative supplementary demand in the 2nd Monsoon Session of June 2026, namely, "35 - Subsidy for Formation of Capital Asset." Accordingly, it was under the consideration of the Government to provide for redistribution under the Subsidy Objective for the creation of a 35-capital fund.
Government Decisions: - Under Jal Jeevan PRA, under the head "General Component" (Scope Category Central Share and State Share), under the head "35-Capital Component" and under the head "Accounts 315 9791 (Central Share 50% - General Component) and" Accounts 2215 2071 (State Share 50% - General Component) respectively.
