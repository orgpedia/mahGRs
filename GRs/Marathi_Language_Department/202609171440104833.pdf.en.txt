Terms and Conditions: -
(1) The All India Marathi Sahitya Parishad shall submit a proposal to the Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board for providing financial assistance to the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan.
(2) The Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board has proposed to provide financial assistance to the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan only after receiving from the All India Marathi Sahitya Nigam Board a sum of Rs. Action should be taken to disburse the amount of financial assistance amounting to Rs.500 crores (literal Rs.5 crores only) to All India Marathi Sahitya Nigam.
(3) The amount of financial assistance to be made available by the Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board for the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan shall be credited to the account of the All India Marathi Sahitya Corporation in lump sum before the tenth day of every year.
In this regard, the Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board should take care to request the details of the proposed conference from the All India Marathi Sahitya Corporation well in advance.
(4) The financial assistance being provided by the Government of Maharashtra shall be utilised only for the various activities to be organized in the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan in the field of Marathi language and literature.
(5) The All India Marathi Sahitya Parishad shall have full responsibility for the proper observance of the rules, regulations and financial matters with respect to the organisation of the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan.
(6) The certificate of appropriation of funds made available by the Government for the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan, the conference minutes, the statement of accounts verified by the Chartered Accountants shall be submitted by the All India Marathi Sahitya Nigam to the Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board under the Government within 1 month after the conference. The Maharashtra State Board of Literature and Culture should also submit a compliance report to the government along with a utility certificate in this regard.
(7) The organizers should be informed by the Maharashtra State Sahitya and Sanskriti Board about the installation of the logo of the Marathi language department along with other placards in the All India Marathi Literary Conference.
(8) The representatives authorised by the State Marathi Development Agency and the Marathi Language Department should be invited to the conference.
(9) The Maharashtra State Board of Literature and Culture shall take care to call for the details of the conference from the concerned body in a time-bound manner.
(10) The completion report regarding the organization of the conference should be submitted to the government within 3 months of receiving the information from the Maharashtra State Literary and Cultural Board, along with the evaluation of the effectiveness of the program as well as the utility certificate.
(11) The Accountant-General, Maharashtra-I, Mumbai or his officers shall have the power to examine all documents relating to the financial assistance sanctioned by the Government for the All India Marathi Sahitya Sammelan and shall submit all documents to him for examination, if so requested.
