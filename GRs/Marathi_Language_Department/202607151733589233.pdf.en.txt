The Proposal: -
The National Centre for the Performing Arts (9970 > 7) will be implementing statewide literature activities in six major divisions of Maharashtra in line with the "Page to Stage" Maharashtra State Literature Dissemination Programme.
The proposed project is aimed at decentralization of material activities and infrastructure up to a regional centre.
It is to deliver quality content programs. The institute will act as the nodal knowledge, curation and documentation partner and will be responsible for conceptual setting, programme planning, coordination with the government, documentation, preparation of activity reports and permanent digital content archival.

Government Decision: - In line with the "Page to Stage" Maharashtra State Literature Dissemination Programme by the National Centre for the Performing Arts (NCPA), state-wide literature activities will be carried out in six major divisions of Maharashtra.
