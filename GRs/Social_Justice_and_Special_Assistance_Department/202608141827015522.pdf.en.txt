the Government Circular
A total of 449 government hostels (boys-231 and girls-218) are operational under the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department. A total of 46,755 students are admitted to these hostels and various necessary facilities are being provided for their education. Instructions have been given to implement the admission THAT under various government decisions referred to above regarding the admission of students to this hostel. Now, under this circular, while implementing this further admission WHAT in the Government Hostels functioning under the Department, it is being directed that "not more than 30 per cent of the seats falling vacant for each Hostel every year shall be allotted to the students / girl students belonging to any one of the Scheduled Castes." Also, while implementing the above admission process, make sure that all the hostel seats are filled in the same year. These instructions should be strictly followed by all concerned.
The circular was posted on the www.maharashtra.gov.in website of the Government of Maharashtra.
