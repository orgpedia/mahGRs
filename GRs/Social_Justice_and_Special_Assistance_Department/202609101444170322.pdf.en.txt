The government's decision:
A total of 117 posts have been sanctioned for the establishment of the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance (itself) Ministry, out of which 6 temporary posts have been made available for the functioning of the National Integration, Universal Fraternity and Ideas Promotion Committee set up under this Department. Orders for extension of these 6 temporary posts are issued separately. A total of 23 posts shown in the return along with the remaining 111 PAKOs are to be continued from date 01.03.2026 to date 31.08.2026 with reference no. 7 here. The approval was granted by a Government Decision dated 10.03.2026.
2. Subject to the conditions contained in the Government Decision, Finance Department No. Post-2016 / Q. No. 8/16 APCCD 31.08.2026, these 23 temporary posts shall now be demerged as indicated in the accompanying return. 01.09.2026 that is. The deadline is being extended to 28.02.2027.
