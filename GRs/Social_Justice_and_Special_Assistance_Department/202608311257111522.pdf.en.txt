The Proposal: -
At present, there are 449 Government Hostels for the education of Backward Class students under Social Justice and Special Assistance Department and 92 Government Residential Schools for the education of SC / ST and newly educated boys and girls. These government hostels have a total intake capacity of 46,663 students and government residential schools have a total intake capacity of 18,600 students. Students admitted to government hostels and government residential schools are fed a nutritious and nutritious diet like breakfast, milk, two meals, boiled eggs, corn CHT for vegetarian children, an apple every day and a regular fruit. Accordingly, the government was considering to approve the minimum bidder (L9) in the e-tendering process by implementing a state-level e-tendering process at the Commissionerate level to waal milk for the admitted students (Flavour Milk Tetra Pack 200 ml) of government hostels and government residential schools in the state under the department.

The Government's Decisions:
The Commissioner, Social Welfare, Pune has submitted a proposal to the Government for approval of the minimum (L1) bidder in the e-tendering process by implementing a state-level e-tendering process at the Commissionerate level for milk WAST for the admitted students (Flavour Milk Tetra Pack 200 ml) of Government hostels and Government Residential School in the State under the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance. In pursuance of this proposal, a meeting of the State Level Administrative Division Procurement Committee under the Chairmanship of the Principal Secretary, Social Justice and Special Assistance Department was held on 18th June, 2026. In these meetings, the proposal was discussed in detail and the State Level Administrative Procurement Committee decided to reduce the minimum (L1) milk supply rate submitted by the tenderer in the e-tendering process to Rs. 26.40 - This rate is set.
