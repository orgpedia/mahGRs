Preamble: The Government decided to start 100 Government Residential Schools for SC and ST and neo-Buddhist boys and girls under the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance. Approval is granted under 01.06.2006.
The government also decided on 1980 new posts for these residential schools. They have been created with the approval of a high-level committee under the 13.04.2011. With a large number of teacher posts lying vacant in this residential school, the Department of School Education has been asked to ensure that there is no academic loss to the admitted children. As per the provision of 27.11.2006, until the appointment of a regular teacher, Reference No. 1B2 The Government decision of the Department of Social Justice has approved the appointment for a period of 19 months on temporary basis on hourly basis. The fixed remuneration for the year 2019-20 is very low compared to the year 2025-26 due to increased inflation in the intervening period. This makes it difficult to find teachers. Also, since the schools of the Social Justice Department are residential in nature, one has to attend the school full-time along with teaching and perform the role of a day teacher. Studies also require the extra hours of a student who has not achieved full potential in that class. In order to improve the educational standard of the school, the government was considering increasing the stipend of hourly teachers in order to get quality and respectable teachers.
