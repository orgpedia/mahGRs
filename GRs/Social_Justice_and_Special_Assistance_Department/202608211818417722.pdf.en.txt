The Proposal: -
In order to achieve greater efficiency in cash management at both Central and State levels, the Government of India has decided to introduce SNA-SPARSH (Timely System of Integrated Real Transfer Time of Integrated Quick Transfers) Alternative Fund Flow Mechanism 991C to disburse funds for Centrally Sponsored Schemes through an integrated framework of Public Financial Management System (PFMS), State Integrated Financial Management Information System (State IFMIS) and Reserve Bank of India's e-Kuber System (e-Kuber).
