Introduction: - Under the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, the backward class boys and girls should be promoted educationally, socially and economically, and should lead a life at par with other sections of the society. Also, Government Hostels under the Department is implemented for the education of backward class boys and girls with the objective of providing all kinds of education and higher education opportunities. At present, there are 449 government hostels for the education of backward class students under the department and 92 government residential schools for the education of SC / ST and neo-Buddhist boys and girls. The government TATA has a total intake capacity of 46,673 students and the government residential schools have a total intake capacity of 18400 students. Government Hostels under Department B Government Residential Schools are doing a commendable job to ensure the economic, social and educational progress of the student enrolled in the Government Residential School.
