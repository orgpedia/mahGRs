Read No. 8 The revised Government Decision is being issued as follows, repealing the Government Decision hereunder.
Under the Central Government's Special Assistance to States for Capital Investments Scheme, interest-free loans of 50 years duration are provided by the Central Government to the States. Under the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investment to the States-2026-2027, the Central Government has sanctioned Part I proposals vide order dated 29/07/2026 and disbursed the first instalment of 66% of these amounts vide order dated 29/07/2026.
2. Now, the issue of allocation of funds distributed by the Central Government in parts to the administrative departments was under the consideration of the Government.
The Government's Decision-
The Government of India is approving the disbursement of funds under Part-I of the Special Assistance to States for Capital Investments Scheme 2026-27 to the respective implementing agencies as under:

Under the Government Decision at Reference No. 6, the Assistant Director, Crushing and Guarantee Control Room, Finance Department has been declared as the Drawing and Disbursing Officer and the Joint Secretary, Finance, Finance Department as the Controlling Officer for the withdrawal of the aforesaid funds from the Subsidy and Accounts Office.
6. The amount sanctioned to be disbursed by the Drawing and Disbursing Officer by the present Government Decision is Rs. 660.00 crore (Part 1) to be transferred to the SNA account mentioned in Annexure-A opened at the Bank of Maharashtra vide Government Decision dated 14th May 2026, drawn from the Subsidy and Accounts Officer, Mumbai. The expenditure meeting in this regard should be conducted by the FAUT B Guarantee Control Room.
7. In pursuance of the guidelines in the letter dated 27.03.2026 dated the Central Government, the Government Decision No. 7 outlines the procedure to be followed by the various administrative departments of the State Government as per the guidelines of the Central Government for availing interest-free loans from the Central Government to the States under the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investments. It is mandatory for the department to take strict action according to those instructions.
8. Funds credited to such SNA bank account should be distributed through the PFMS system in a centralized manner to the beneficiaries selected by the respective implementing agencies at their level. In this regard, appropriate action should be taken to disburse the sanctioned amount through PEMS system as per the guidelines of the Government of India dated 14.5.2026 and dated 9.7.2026 of the Department of Finance.
9. In order to receive the second instalment of funds from the Central Government, the departments concerned shall submit to the Finance Department a certificate of expenditure in the form of GFR 127 of the Central Government in the form of at least 75% of the amount in Figure No. 5 of the above table before 05.09.2026 with the signature of the A.M.S. / Principal Secretary / Secretary of the concerned department. However, the department should note that all remaining funds will have to be fully spent by 31 March 2027.
10. Funds received from the Central Government shall be deemed to be expenditure only after they are withdrawn from the treasury and provided to the final beneficiaries concerned. It should be noted that funds withdrawn from the treasury are not allowed to be stored in other accounts.
11. While spending funds received from the Central Government, all the departments are required to ensure that no double funding is provided from other funds of the Central or State Government for the same matter or from funds received in other areas in the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investments to the States. It may be noted that such a certificate has already been submitted to the Central Government with the signature of the Secretary of the Department.
12. Detailed project reports of all projects sanctioned by the Central Government must be available together in the Department's archives. For this, the department needs to appoint a nodal officer to ensure that all records and payments are available. An audit in this regard is likely to be conducted by the Central Government through the Accountant General at any time. It will be the responsibility of the department concerned to make all the records available to them.
13. Government Decision, Department of Finance / Expenditure 7 Non-formal reference no. 499 of the Executive, d. Is being issued according to the approval received under 3.8.2026.
