Background:
The Government of Maharashtra has actively pursued investment-led growth with a
specific focus on inclusive social development, and has adopted the "Viksit Maharashtra @
2047 "Vision approved by the Council of Ministers on 28th October, 2025, with milestones for
The years 2030, for 2035 and for the centenary year of India's independence. As the state works towards its goal of becoming an I trillion-dollar economy by 2030 and a 5 trillion-dollar economy
by 2047, it 1s necessary to create a growth-oriented and fiscally responsible roadmap for
government finances that sustains growth opportunities and caters to all stakeholders, especially
the youth.
2. The delivery of this Vision requires a sustained increase in public investment in
infrastructure, human capital, water security, urban systems and the energy transition, over and
above the State's growing commitments on salaries, pensions, interests and social welfare. The
State has consistently observed the targets prescribed under the Maharashtra Fiscal
Responsibility and Budgetary Management Act, 2005 and intends to continue to do so. Within
That discipline, the fiscal space available for development is determined in principle by the strength of the state's own revenue effort and by the efficiency with which its expenditure is
allocated and executed.
# Page 8
3. Borrowed funds can be used to bridge deficits, and to finance investment projects and
relieve temporary financing constraints. But using borrowed funds to bridge a structural gap
between recurring expenses and recurring revenues 15 neither prudent nor advisable. Hence the
resources required to fulfil the vision must be raised on a foundation of durable improvement in
tax and non-tax revenues.
Accordingly, to align the growth objectives of the economy with commensurate growth in
resources requirement, the Government is pleased to issue the following resolution.
GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION:
Government hereby constitutes a Committee to be called the "Maharashtra Committee."
on Sustainable Public Finance "(hereinafter" the Committee ") to recommend measures for the
Sustainable augmentation of the state's own tax and non-tax revenues and for the consolidation
of its fiscal position, by improving compliance, plugging leakages, rationalising rates, fees and
exemptions, strengthening returns from public assets and enterprises, identifying new and under-
tapped sources of revenue, and improving the efficiency of public expenditure.

4. Terms of Reference:-
(1) To study the buoyancy and stability of the state's tax revenues including the various
Components of tax revenues covering among others GST and VAT on petroleum and liquor - and to recommend measures to improve tax collection through the linking of various taxes and
administrative databases and the use of analytics and risk-based enforcement; and, in particular,
to recommend ways of enhancing revenue from alcohol for human consumption through changes in the regulation and taxation of potable alcohol, having regard to public health and enforcement
considerations.
(11) To identify new and under-tapped non-tax revenue sources and to make specific
suggestions for their enhancement, including among others dividends and other returns from State public sector undertakings, royalties and dead rent from major and minor minerals, public-
private partnerships and user charges; and to recommend a framework for the periodic review of
user charges, royalties, and fees against the cost of the services and assets to which they relate.
(111) To assess, in consultation with the Planning Department and the Vision Management
Unit, the resource envelope implied by the Viksit Maharashtra @ 2047 Vision over its three
milestones; to recommend a Medium Term Revenue Strategy for the State for the award period
of the Sixteenth Finance Commission and a long term strategy as laid out in the Vision.
(iv) To examine financial liquidity management within the state's public financial
management system and to suggest ways of improving its efficiency, and to advise on increasing the allocative and quality of government   expenditure, including through outcome budgeting,
particularly in the infrastructure and social sectors.
(v) To examine the classification, definition and presentation of government revenue in the
State's budget and accounts and examine the adoption of internationally accepted standards,
including those of the Government Finance Statistics Manual, 2014, so as to introduce clarity to
the reporting of the state's public finances; and to review and suggest an appropriate framework
for fiscal responsibility legislation for Maharashtra that ensures a sustainable trajectory of high
and inclusive growth.
(v1) To suggest such organisational and institutional reforms as would improve efficiency
and transparency, including a permanent capacity within the Finance Department for revenue
forecasting, tax expenditure analysis and revenue research; to examine the own revenues of urban
and rural local bodies and development authorities, including property tax, development charges
and land value capture, to the extent that these bear upon the state's fiscal transfers and
contingent liabilities; and to consider any other matter incidental or germane to the above or referred to the Committee by Government.
5. Process and Procedure.
(1) Meetings and consultation. The Committee shall meet at the direction of the
Chairperson, and may meet in person or through video conference. It may hold consultations at Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur or elsewhere, and may consult trade and industry associations, professional bodies, taxpayers, academic institutions and the general public. It may co-opt, or invite as special invitees, Heads of Departments, field officers or subject experts for specific
items on its agenda, without their becoming permanent Members.
(11) Power to call for information. The Committee is empowered to call for any record, return, data or report from any Department, Public Sector Undertaking, Board, Authority, local
# Page 10
body or revenue-collecting agency of the State, and to require officers to furnish information in person. Departments and agencies shall comply within the time stipulated by the Committee, and shall on request provide anonymised unit-level and transaction-level data required for its
analysis, subject to the applicable provisions of law relating to confidentiality of taxpayer
information. Members of the Committee, the personnel of its Secretariat and all persons associated with its work shall maintain the confidentiality of such information and use it solely
for the purposes of the Committee.
(111) Sub-groups. The Committee may constitute sub-groups or working groups for individual revenue heads or subjects, with defined terms of reference and timelines - including
for Goods and Services Tax and Value Added Tax; Registration and Stamps; State Excise; Motor
Vehicles Tax; Energy and Electricity Duty; Mines and Minerals; non-tax revenue, public sector undertaking dividends and asset monetisation; and expenditure efficiency and fiscal reporting.
(iv) Secretariat. The Pune International Centre, Pune shall provide the Secretariat to the
Committee, and shall convene its meetings, maintain its records, coordinate with Departments
and agencies, commission and compile background material and prepare the drafts of its reports.
The   Centre for Effective Governance of Indian States (CEGIS) shall assist the Secretariat with
analytical, data and research support, including the assembly and analysis of administrative data
from the   revenue-collecting departments. The Finance Department shall be the administrative
Department for the Committee and shall enter into a memorandum of understanding with each
institution setting out the scope of the support, the arrangements for the custody, security and disposal of data, and the confidentiality obligations of the personnel deployed.
(v) Reporting and tenure. The Committee shall report to Government in the Finance
Department and shall submit its report, with specific, prioritised and _ time-bound
recommendations, within a period of six months from the date of this Government Resolution; it
may submit an interim report at any time. The report shall, for each recommendation, indicate the Department concerned, the instrument required to give it effect, the indicative fiscal implication
and the timeline for implementation. The Finance Department shall track the action taken by
Departments on the recommendations of the Committee. The committee may, if so desired, suggest the mechanism for an annual appraisal of the implementation of its recommendations.
