10. Funds received from the Central Government shall be deemed to be deficit only after they are withdrawn from the treasury and provided to the final beneficiaries concerned. It should be noted that funds withdrawn from the treasury are not allowed to be stored in other accounts.
11 While spending funds received from the Central Government, all the departments are required to ensure that no funds are provided twice from other funds of the Central or State Government for the same purpose or from funds received elsewhere in the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investments to the States. It may be noted that such a certificate has already been submitted to the Central Government with the signature of the Secretary of the Department.
12. Loan departments sanctioned by the Central Government shall spend within the sanctioned limits on the same projects for which they have been sanctioned. In some unavoidable circumstances, prior approval of the Central Government is required to make changes. The departments should, therefore, submit a proposal of change to the finance department with clear reasons.
13. Detailed project reports of all projects sanctioned by the Central Government must be available together in the records of the Department. For this, the department needs to appoint a nodal officer to ensure that all records and payments are available. An audit in this regard is likely to be conducted by the Central Government through the Accountant General at any time. It will be the responsibility of the department concerned to make all the records available to them.
14. All guidelines of the Central Government shall be strictly followed accordingly.
