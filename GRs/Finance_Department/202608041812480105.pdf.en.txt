Since the funds are disbursed as loans to the City and Industrial Development Corporation (CIDCO), the terms and conditions of the loan need to be determined. For this, the department will be required to issue separate government decisions in this regard from their level.
8. In pursuance of the guidelines in the letter dated 27.03.2026 dated the Central Government, the Government Decision No. 7 outlines the procedure to be followed by the various administrative departments of the State Government for availing interest-free loans under the Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment Scheme from the Central Government as per the guidelines of the Central Government. It is mandatory for the department to take strict action according to those instructions.
9. The funds deposited in such SNA bank account should be distributed centrally through PEMS system to the beneficiaries selected by the respective implementing agencies at their level. In this regard, appropriate action should be taken to disburse the sanctioned amount through the PFMS system as per the guidelines of the Government of India dated 14.5.2026 and dated 9.7.2026 of the Department of Finance.
In order to receive the second instalment of funds from the Central Government, the departments concerned shall submit to the Department of Finance a certificate of expenditure in the form of GER 123 of the Central Government in the form of at least 75% of the amount in Table No. 5, dated 05.09.2026 with the signature of the Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary to the Department concerned. However, the department should note that all remaining funds will have to be fully spent by 31 March 2027.
119 Funds received from the Central Government will be treated as balance only after it is withdrawn from the treasury and provided to the final beneficiaries concerned. It should be noted that funds withdrawn from the treasury are not allowed to be stored in other accounts.
12. While spending funds received from the Central Government, all the departments are required to ensure that no double funding is provided from other funds of the Central or State Government for the same matter or from funds received in other areas in the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investment to the States. It may be noted that such a certificate has already been submitted to the Central Government with the signature of the Secretary of the Department.
13. Detailed project reports of survey projects sanctioned by the Central Government must be available together in the Department's archives. For this, the department has appointed a nodal officer to keep all the records.
