In order to receive the second instalment of funds from the Central Government, the departments concerned shall submit to the Finance Department a certificate of expenditure in the form of GER 127 of the Central Government in the form of at least 75% of the amount in Figure No. 5 in the above table before 05.09.2026 with the signature of the A.S. / Principal Secretary / Secretary of the concerned department. However, the department should note that all remaining funds will have to be fully spent by 31 APT 2027.
9. Funds received from the Central Government shall be deemed to be deficit only after they are withdrawn from the treasury and provided to the final beneficiaries concerned. It should be noted that funds withdrawn from the treasury are not allowed to be stored in other accounts.
10. While spending funds received from the Central Government, all the departments are required to ensure that no funds are provided twice from other funds of the Central or State Government for the same purpose or from funds received elsewhere in the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investments to the States. It may be noted that such a certificate has already been submitted to the Central Government with the signature of the Secretary of the Department.
19. Loan departments sanctioned by the Central Government shall reduce the sanctioned limit on the same projects for which they have been sanctioned. In some unavoidable circumstances, prior approval of the Central Government is required to make changes. The departments should, therefore, submit a proposal of change to the finance department with clear reasons.
