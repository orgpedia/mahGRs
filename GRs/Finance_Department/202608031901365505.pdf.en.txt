While spending funds received from the Central Government, all the departments are required to ensure that no double funding is provided from other funds of the Central or State Government for the same matter or from funds received in other areas in the Special Assistance Scheme for Capital Investments to the States. 12. Loan departments sanctioned by the Central Government shall reduce the sanctioned limit on the same projects for which they have been sanctioned. In some unavoidable circumstances, prior approval of the Central Government is required to make changes. The departments should, therefore, submit a proposal of change to the finance department with clear reasons.
13. Detailed project reports of all projects sanctioned by the Central Government must be available together in the records of the Department.
