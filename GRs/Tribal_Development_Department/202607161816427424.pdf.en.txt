On the constitution of a committee on the facilities available to persons belonging to the Scheduled Tribes after conversion.
Government of Maharashtra
Tribal Development Department
Government Decision Number: STC-2525 / Q. No.329 / Q.10
Madam Cama Road, Martyr Rajguru Chowk,
Mantralaya, Mumbai - 400 032
Date: July 16, 2026
Preamble: In the second (monsoon) session of the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly in 2025, Sarvashri. Anupbhai Agarwal et al., Hon'ble V.S., made a notable suggestion on the subject "Forcing conversion by inducing tribal and non-tribal people and their Christian clerics, missionaries and their institutions in Navapur Taluka of Nandurbar District" vide Notice No. 1773 was presented.
The relevant honor during this attention-grabbing GAR test. The member of the Legislative Assembly had raised the issue of "cancellation of concessions taken by the tribal or scheduled caste community after conversion." In this regard, Shri. Minister, Revenue and M.S. The Minister for Tribal Development had assured that a committee of members of the Legislative Assembly from the Scheduled Tribes would be constituted to deliberate on the matter.
In pursuance of these assurances, the government was considering setting up a committee to study the laws of the central and state governments and formulate a comprehensive policy for the state on the benefits of conversion by persons belonging to Scheduled Tribes.
The Government's Decisions:
The following committee is being constituted to prepare a comprehensive policy for the state after studying the laws of the Central and other state governments regarding the facilities available to the persons belonging to the Scheduled Tribes after conversion.
